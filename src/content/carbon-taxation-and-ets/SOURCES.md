# Carbon Pricing Course Sources

## Primary Sources (Downloaded)

### 1. WorldBank_PMR_Assessment_Guide_2021.pdf (3.0 MB)
**Carbon Pricing Assessment and Decision-Making: A Guide to Adopting a Carbon Price**
- Publisher: World Bank / Partnership for Market Readiness (PMR)
- Date: 2021
- Structure: 4 chapters + 7 attachments
  - Ch 1: Introduction to carbon pricing
  - Ch 2: The jurisdictional context
  - Ch 3: Impact assessment
  - Ch 4: Reaching a recommendation
  - Attachments: Policy interactions, Questionnaire, Data tip sheet, Capacity assessment, Modeling summary, Example TOR, Recommendation paper best practice

### 2. WorldBank_Carbon_Tax_Guide_2017.pdf (5.9 MB)
**Carbon Tax Guide: A Handbook for Policy Makers**
- Publisher: World Bank / PMR / Climate Focus
- Date: March 2017
- Structure: 10 chapters + appendices
  - Synthesis: Carbon Taxes in Brief
  - Ch 2: Deciding Whether to Adopt a Carbon Tax
  - Ch 3: Preparing for Carbon Tax Adoption
  - Ch 4: Modeling Carbon Taxes
  - Ch 5: Defining the Tax Base
  - Ch 6: Determining the Tax Rate
  - Ch 7: Avoiding Unwanted Effects
  - Ch 8: Determining Use of Revenues
  - Ch 9: Ensuring Oversight and Compliance
  - Ch 10: Evaluating Policy Outcomes

### 3. ICAP_ETS_Handbook_2021.pdf (7.9 MB)
**Emissions Trading in Practice: A Handbook on Design and Implementation (2nd Edition)**
- Publisher: ICAP / World Bank / PMR
- Date: 2021
- Structure: 10 steps
  - Step 1: Prepare
  - Step 2: Engage stakeholders, communicate, and build capacity
  - Step 3: Decide the scope
  - Step 4: Set the cap
  - Step 5: Distribute allowances
  - Step 6: Promote a well-functioning market
  - Step 7: Ensure compliance and oversight
  - Step 8: Consider the use of offsets
  - Step 9: Consider linking
  - Step 10: Implement, evaluate, and improve

### 4. WorldBank_State_Trends_2024.pdf (7.2 MB)
**State and Trends of Carbon Pricing 2024**
- Publisher: World Bank
- Date: August 2024
- Key data: 75 carbon pricing instruments globally, coverage and price analysis

### 5. WorldBank_Communicating_Carbon_Pricing.pdf (3.6 MB)
**Guide to Communicating Carbon Pricing**
- Publisher: World Bank / PMR / CPLC
- Date: 2018
- Focus: Stakeholder engagement, public communication, political feasibility

### 6. OECD_Pricing_GHG_Emissions_2024.pdf (3.3 MB)
**Pricing Greenhouse Gas Emissions 2024**
- Publisher: OECD
- Date: November 2024
- Coverage: 79 countries, Net Effective Carbon Rates analysis

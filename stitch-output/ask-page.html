Summarize the core impact of the 2024 Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) on mid-sized manufacturing firms in the EU.

temp_preferences_custom

CSRD Impact Analysis

The 2024 CSRD fundamentally shifts reporting from voluntary frameworks to mandatory, audited disclosures. For mid-sized manufacturing entities, this implies three primary structural changes:

  • 1

    Double Materiality Mandate

    Firms must now report on how sustainability issues affect their business Ref: Art. 19a and how their operations impact the planet.

  • 2

    Value Chain Transparency

    Scope 3 emissions reporting is no longer optional, requiring intensive data gathering from Tier 2 and Tier 3 suppliers Ref: ESRS E1.

"The directive effectively treats sustainability data with the same rigor as financial data, mandating third-party assurance."

The Three Pillars of ESG

To understand ESG, one must look at the structural framework that supports sustainable business. Rather than treating sustainability as a single initiative, the ESG framework breaks down corporate responsibility into three distinct, measurable categories.

lightbulb

Key takeaway

ESG covers environmental, social, and governance factors, creating a holistic view of a company's long-term viability and risk profile beyond simple financial metrics.

Environmental Stewardship

This pillar evaluates how a company acts as a steward of the physical environment, including climate change, resource depletion, and biodiversity loss.

ABSTRACT

This reference library curates 211,651 greenhouse gas emission factors drawn from 41 primary regulatory and scientific sources, including IPCC, IEA, and national environmental protection agencies. Each entry includes full metadata, verified uncertainty ranges, and original source documentation links to ensure auditability for corporate sustainability reporting and life cycle assessment. Access is free for practitioner use.

C

Carbon Credit

Carbon Markets

A tradable certificate or permit representing the right to emit one tonne of carbon dioxide or the equivalent amount of a different greenhouse gas (tCO2e).

Circular Economy

Circularity

A model of production and consumption which involves sharing, leasing, reusing, repairing, refurbishing and recycling existing materials and products as long as possible.

View Related Courses arrow_forward

CSRD

Reporting Standards

Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive. An EU regulation requiring large companies and listed SMEs to publish regular reports on their social and environmental impact.

D

Double Materiality

ESG

A reporting principle that requires companies to disclose both how sustainability issues affect their business (financial materiality) and how their business affects people and the environment (impact materiality).

View Related Courses arrow_forward
E

ESRS

Reporting Standards

European Sustainability Reporting Standards. The technical standards used by companies to comply with the CSRD reporting requirements.

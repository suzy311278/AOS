The Glasgow Rulebook

Article 6 of the Paris Agreement provides a framework for countries to cooperate in meeting their NDCs through international carbon markets. The "Glasgow Rulebook," agreed at COP26, finally established the operational guidelines for these mechanisms, ensuring environmental integrity and avoiding double counting.

lightbulb

Key Takeaway

The Glasgow Rulebook prohibits "Double Counting"—ensuring that a single emission reduction cannot be claimed by both the buying and selling country.

The Symbiosis of Urban Forestry

Urban forests are complex ecosystems that provide critical services to city residents. From mitigating the heat island effect to improving air quality, the presence of trees in metropolitan areas is no longer just an aesthetic choice but a public health necessity.

Recent studies have shown that neighborhoods with a 30% canopy cover report significantly lower rates of respiratory illnesses. Furthermore, the psychological impact of green spaces contributes to a 15% reduction in stress-related markers among frequent visitors.

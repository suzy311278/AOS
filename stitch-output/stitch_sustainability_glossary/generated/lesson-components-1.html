SOC Stock Formula

SOC Stock = OC × BD × D × 0.1
science OC: Organic Carbon

The concentration of carbon measured in the soil laboratory, typically expressed as g/kg.

layers BD: Bulk Density

The mass of dry soil per unit volume, accounting for pore space, expressed in g/cm³.

straighten D: Depth

The vertical thickness of the soil layer being measured, typically in centimeters (cm).

calculate 0.1: Conversion

A constant factor used to convert various units into the final metric of tonnes per hectare (tC/ha).

SOC stock = 18 g/kg x 1.35 g/cm3 x 30 cm x 0.1 = 72.9 tC/ha
Variable What It Is How Measured
Bulk Density Dry soil weight per unit volume Core sampler extraction
Organic Carbon Carbon concentration by mass Dumas combustion method
Rock Fragment Volume of stones > 2mm Sieving and water displacement
lightbulb

Key Takeaway

The SOC stock is the only metric that accounts for the actual mass of carbon in the ecosystem, rather than just the concentration percentage.

compare_arrows

Analogy: Roads to the same destination

Measuring carbon concentration without bulk density is like counting the percentage of red cars on a road without knowing how many total lanes or cars there are.

edit_note Worked Example:

Calculate the carbon stock for a pasture site with the following metrics:

  • Lab Carbon (OC): 22 g/kg
  • Bulk Density (BD): 1.1 g/cm³
  • Sampling Depth (D): 0-15 cm

Calculation: 22 × 1.1 × 15 × 0.1 = 36.3 tC/ha

biotech Want to go deeper? Why stratified random sampling? expand_more

Stratification reduces the variance within sampling groups, allowing for a more accurate global estimate with fewer total samples.

  • Groups are defined by soil type, topography, and land-use history.
  • Ensures "hotspots" don't skew the average for the entire project area.

The 3-Step Process:

  1. Overlay soil maps with digital elevation models.
  2. Divide the landscape into "Strata" based on similarity.
  3. Randomly assign sample locations within each unique stratum.

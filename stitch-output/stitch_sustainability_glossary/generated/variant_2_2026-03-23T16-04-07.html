SOC Stock Formula

Mastering soil organic carbon calculations for environmental verification roles.

SOC Stock = OC × BD × D × 0.1
experiment OC (Organic Carbon)

The concentration of organic carbon in the soil sample, typically expressed in g/kg or percentage.

layers BD (Bulk Density)

The dry weight of soil per unit volume (g/cm³). Critical for converting concentration to mass.

straighten D (Depth)

The thickness of the soil layer being measured (cm). Standard depths are 0-30cm or 0-100cm.

calculate 0.1 (Conversion)

A mathematical constant used to convert the final units into tonnes of Carbon per hectare (tC/ha).

SOC stock = 18 g/kg x 1.35 g/cm³ x 30 cm x 0.1 = 72.9 tC/ha

tips_and_updates Key Takeaway

Small changes in Organic Carbon (OC) or Bulk Density (BD) can lead to massive differences in total carbon stocks when scaled to the hectare level. Accurate lab testing is non-negotiable for project verification.

psychology Analogy: Roads to the same destination

Think of SOC stock as the total volume of traffic on a highway. The OC is the percentage of electric vehicles, while BD is the total number of lanes. You need both numbers to understand the true impact on emissions.

calculate Worked Example:

Imagine a degraded pasture in Brazil being restored through silvopasture:

  • Initial OC 12 g/kg
  • Bulk Density 1.45 g/cm³
  • Sampling Depth 30 cm

Calculation: 12 x 1.45 x 30 x 0.1 = 52.2 tC/ha

Note: After 5 years of restoration, if OC rises to 15 g/kg, the stock becomes 65.25 tC/ha.

Soil isn't uniform. Topography, vegetation, and history create "patches" of carbon. Stratified sampling ensures we don't overestimate based on one fertile spot.

  1. 1

    Divide the project area into "strata" based on soil type or land use.

  2. 2

    Assign a number of sample plots proportional to the area of each stratum.

  3. 3

    Randomly locate plots within each stratum to avoid bias.

Course Overview

Article 6 of the Paris Agreement is arguably the most complex and least understood element of the global climate regime. It governs how countries can cooperate to reach their climate targets, primarily through carbon markets and non-market approaches.

This course provides a comprehensive analytical framework for understanding Article 6.2, 6.4, and 6.8, moving from the foundational legal text to the latest implementation rules established at COP26 and beyond. You will explore double counting, additionality, and the "Corresponding Adjustments" mechanism that ensures environmental integrity.

Understanding the Mechanisms

Article 6 of the Paris Agreement outlines how countries can pursue voluntary cooperation in the implementation of their Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) to allow for higher ambition in their mitigation and adaptation actions.

It provides a framework for international carbon markets, establishing two key mechanisms:

  • eco
    Article 6.2 (Cooperative Approaches) Allows countries to trade emission reductions and removals with one another through bilateral or multilateral agreements. These traded credits are known as Internationally Transferred Mitigation Outcomes (ITMOs).
  • public
    Article 6.4 (The Mechanism) Establishes a centralized, multilateral carbon crediting mechanism overseen by a UN entity. It replaces the Kyoto Protocol's Clean Development Mechanism (CDM), allowing public and private entities to trade emissions reductions.

Key Takeaway

The overarching goal of Article 6 is not just to make it cheaper to achieve existing targets, but to facilitate higher ambition overall, ensuring a net reduction in global emissions.

A

Additionality

Carbon Markets

The requirement that a carbon offset project results in emission reductions or removals that would not have occurred in the absence of the project activity. Additionality ensures that carbon credits represent genuine climate benefits beyond the business-as-usual scenario.

Related: Baseline Scenario , Carbon Credit , Crediting Period

C

Carbon Credit

Carbon Markets

A tradeable certificate or permit representing the right to emit one tonne of carbon dioxide equivalent (tCO2e). Carbon credits are generated by projects that reduce, avoid, or remove greenhouse gas emissions from the atmosphere.

Related: Additionality , ITMO , Corresponding Adjustment

Corresponding Adjustment

GHG Protocol

An accounting entry applied by a country that transfers Internationally Transferred Mitigation Outcomes (ITMOs) to avoid double counting. The transferring country adds the emissions back to its national inventory, while the acquiring country subtracts them from its own.

Related: ITMO , NDC , Double Counting

I

ITMO

Climate Science

Internationally Transferred Mitigation Outcome. A unit of emission reduction or removal that is transferred between countries under Article 6.2 of the Paris Agreement. ITMOs enable countries to cooperate in achieving their Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) through international carbon markets.

Related: Corresponding Adjustment , NDC , Article 6

N

NDC

Climate Science

Nationally Determined Contribution. A climate action plan submitted by each country under the Paris Agreement, outlining its targets for reducing greenhouse gas emissions and adapting to climate change. NDCs are updated every five years with progressively more ambitious targets.

Related: ITMO , Paris Agreement , Corresponding Adjustment

S

Scope 1 Emissions

GHG Protocol

Direct greenhouse gas emissions from sources that are owned or controlled by the reporting organization. This includes emissions from combustion in owned or controlled boilers, furnaces, and vehicles, as well as emissions from chemical production in owned or controlled process equipment.

Related: Scope 2 Emissions , Scope 3 Emissions , GHG Protocol

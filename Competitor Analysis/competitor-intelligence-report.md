## Competitor Summaries

- **Type:** Paid CPD-certified course platform ($420-$590/course)
- **Operator:** Centre for Sustainability & Excellence (CSE)
- **Scale:** 21 courses, 25,000+ professionals trained, 90+ countries
- **Model:** Self-paced video, 20-25 hours, 45-day window, quiz + assignment + certificate
- **Accreditations:** CPD, CMI, GRI Training Partner, PRI Member, SASB Partner

**Course catalog:**
- Corporate Sustainability Diploma (flagship, also in Spanish/French)
- ESG Reporting (GRI, CSRD/ESRS) - also in Spanish
- Carbon Reduction & Net Zero Strategies
- CSRD & ESRS Standards
- IFRS S1 & S2
- SASB & TCFD Reporting (aligned to IFRS)
- ESG Ratings & Investments
- Circular Economy Professional
- Social Impact Assessment & SROI
- Social Entrepreneurship
- Green/Sustainable Marketing
- Sustainable Supply Chain Management
- AI for Sustainability (AIBIZ)
- GenAI for Business (GenAIBIZ)
- Climate Resilient Officer (CRO)
- Renewable Energy Fundamentals
- ESG for CFOs/IRs/Fund Managers
- Sustainability for Yachting Professionals

### 2. Persefoni Academy (persefoni.com)
- **Type:** Free lead-gen courses for their SaaS platform
- **Scale:** Only 4 courses (thin educational layer)
- **Model:** Free sign-up, Easy LMS, no certificates
- **Real product:** Carbon accounting SaaS (free tier + enterprise)

**Courses:** Carbon Accounting Essentials, Decarbonization 101, Scope 3 101, California Climate Disclosure Compliance

### 3. SDG Academy (sdgacademy.org)
- **Type:** Free MOOCs via edX
- **Operator:** UN SDSN, led by Jeffrey Sachs
- **Scale:** 50+ MOOCs, 2,000+ videos, Creative Commons licensed
- **Model:** Free audit, paid verified certificates via edX, Master's pathway with UCD

**Key courses:** Climate Change Science, Climate Action Solutions, Nature-based Solutions, Ecosystem-based Adaptation, Measuring Sustainable Development, Ethics in Action, Early Childhood Development, Gender Equality, Living Heritage, MEL for NAP, Green Jobs

### 4. Verst Carbon (verst.earth)
- **Type:** Carbon project developer with e-learning as side offering
- **Scale:** E-learning appears early-stage; real strength is bespoke workshops
- **Model:** Instructor-led workshops with institutional partners

**Course topics:** Climate Change Fundamentals, Carbon Markets Explained, Kyoto/Paris Agreement, Legal Aspects of Carbon Markets, Carbon Trading in Finance, Engineering Solutions for Carbon Reduction

### 5. The ESG Institute (the-esg-institute.org)
- **Type:** Professional body + course provider ($199-$399)
- **Scale:** 8 active courses + 10 on waiting list, 7,000+ members, 70+ countries
- **Model:** Self-paced on Teachable, professional membership tiers, accreditation body

**Active courses:** ESG Strategy Diploma, Carbon Markets & Climate Finance Diploma, Sustainable Finance Diploma, UAE Climate Law Diploma, CBAM Regulation Diploma, CSRD Professional Certificate, ESG Reporting Certificate, Sustainable Technology Certificate

**Coming soon:** Circular Economy, ESG Investing, Supply Chain, AI & Sustainability, Saudi Corporate Sustainability, Sustainable Project Management

### 6. KnowESG (knowesg.com)
- **Type:** Aggregator/directory (does NOT create courses)
- **Scale:** 146+ courses from 84 providers listed
- **Model:** Directory with filters; monetizes via featured listings, ESG ratings database, solutions marketplace

**Categories:** ESG Foundations (35), Environmental & Climate (36), Sustainable Technology (40), Sustainable Finance (25), Social & Governance (7), Nature & Biodiversity (2)

### 7. UNEP FI (unepfi.org)
- **Type:** UN membership org for finance sector
- **Scale:** 550+ member institutions, $170T+ in assets
- **Model:** Membership-gated peer learning, webinars, research programmes, framework implementation support

**Training:** PRB Academy, ESRA Online Course, Climate Change Risk Management Course, Risk Centre Research Programme, CSRD/ESRS Implementation Support, Investment Leadership Programme

## Gap Analysis - What We DON'T Cover That Competitors Do

### HIGH-PRIORITY GAPS (multiple competitors cover, high market demand)

1. **Circular Economy**
   - Covered by: sustainability-academy.org (certificate), ESG Institute (coming), KnowESG (category)
   - Market signal: Strong demand, EU Circular Economy Action Plan driving corporate interest
   - Recommendation: Create a dedicated course

2. **Climate Risk & Resilience / TCFD**
   - Covered by: sustainability-academy.org (CRO cert), UNEP FI (banking course), SDG Academy
   - Market signal: TCFD merged into ISSB, but climate risk assessment is a core skill
   - Recommendation: Add climate risk module to IFRS S2 course OR create standalone

3. **ESG Reporting Fundamentals (GRI Standards)**
   - Covered by: sustainability-academy.org (flagship), ESG Institute (certificate)
   - Market signal: GRI is the most-used reporting framework globally
   - Recommendation: Create a GRI Standards course - this is a major gap

4. **Decarbonization Strategy / Net Zero Roadmaps**
   - Covered by: sustainability-academy.org (certificate), Persefoni (101 course), UNEP FI
   - Market signal: Every company setting net-zero targets needs this skill
   - Recommendation: Create course covering target-setting, roadmap design, technology pathways

5. **CSRD/ESRS Deep-Dive**
   - Our Double Materiality course covers one piece; competitors offer full CSRD courses
   - Covered by: sustainability-academy.org, ESG Institute (certificate), UNEP FI
   - Recommendation: Expand into full CSRD/ESRS reporting course

6. **EU CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism)**
   - Covered by: ESG Institute (diploma), UNEP FI (webinars)
   - Market signal: CBAM is now in force, affecting importers globally
   - Recommendation: Standalone short course or module within carbon markets

### MEDIUM-PRIORITY GAPS (emerging topics, differentiation opportunity)

7. **AI for Sustainability / ESG**
   - Covered by: sustainability-academy.org (2 courses), ESG Institute (coming)
   - Market signal: Rapidly growing intersection; few quality courses exist
   - Recommendation: Course on AI tools for carbon accounting, ESG data, reporting automation

8. **Biodiversity & Nature (TNFD)**
   - Covered by: SDG Academy, UNEP FI, KnowESG (2 courses listed)
   - Market signal: TNFD launched, EU Biodiversity Strategy, growing regulatory pressure
   - Recommendation: Create TNFD/biodiversity course - almost no competitors cover this well

9. **Supply Chain Sustainability**
   - Covered by: sustainability-academy.org (diploma), ESG Institute (coming)
   - Market signal: EU CSDDD, German Supply Chain Act, US forced labor rules
   - Recommendation: Course on supply chain due diligence, Scope 3 supplier engagement

10. **Sustainable Finance Overview**
    - We have SFDR, PCAF, ESG Investing as separate courses but no unified intro
    - Covered by: ESG Institute (diploma), UNEP FI (comprehensive)
    - Recommendation: Consider a "Sustainable Finance 101" gateway course

### LOWER-PRIORITY / NICHE GAPS

11. **Social Impact Assessment / SROI** - sustainability-academy.org covers this; niche but growing
12. **Green Marketing / Greenwashing** - sustainability-academy.org; relevant for comms professionals
13. **Climate Science Fundamentals** - SDG Academy; we could add as an introductory/gateway course
14. **Region-Specific Regulation** (California SB 253/261, UAE, Saudi) - Persefoni, ESG Institute cover; niche
15. **Renewable Energy Fundamentals** - sustainability-academy.org; could complement our climate courses

1. **100% free** - Only SDG Academy matches this. sustainability-academy.org charges $420-590, ESG Institute $199-399
2. **Deep technical depth** - Our VM0042/VM0044 methodology courses and Scope 3 calculations go deeper than any competitor
3. **Interactive learning** - Quizzes, calculation exercises, equation breakdowns, mermaid diagrams - richer than video-only competitors
4. **Source-backed content** - Every lesson references specific PDF sections; competitors rarely cite sources
5. **Niche carbon methodology expertise** - No competitor covers Verra methodologies at our depth
6. **No paywall, no registration** - Instant access vs. sign-up flows and payment gates
7. **Open-source, self-hosted** - Full transparency; competitors are closed platforms

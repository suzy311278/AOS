## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is sustainable finance software?
▼
Sustainable finance software helps asset managers and investors integrate ESG factors into investment decisions, calculate portfolio carbon footprints, comply with SFDR regulations, and measure impact across environmental and social dimensions.
### How much does ESG investment software cost?
▼
ESG investment platforms range from $10,000-$200,000/year depending on AUM and features. Basic ESG screening tools start at $5,000/year, while enterprise portfolio analytics with SFDR compliance can exceed $150,000/year.
### What is SFDR and how do I comply?
▼
The Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation (SFDR) requires EU financial market participants to disclose sustainability risks and impacts. Software helps classify funds (Article 6/8/9), generate Principal Adverse Impact statements, and produce required disclosures.
### How do I calculate portfolio carbon footprint?
▼
Portfolio carbon analytics tools calculate financed emissions using PCAF methodology—measuring Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions attributed to your investments based on ownership share. Leading platforms provide attribution analysis and decarbonization pathways.
### What is impact measurement in sustainable investing?
▼
Impact measurement quantifies the real-world environmental and social outcomes of investments beyond financial returns. Software tracks metrics like tons of CO2 avoided, jobs created, or clean energy generated, aligned with frameworks like IRIS+ and UN SDGs.

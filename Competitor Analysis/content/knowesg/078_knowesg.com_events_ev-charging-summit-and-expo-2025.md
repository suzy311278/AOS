## _Decarbonising Transport Event to Take Place in Las Vegas (March 17–19, 2026)_
**Event Description**
Las Vegas is inviting everyone to attend the leading event for the electrification of transportation. Attendees can access several educational sessions, from financing and infrastructure to operability and ROI for EV charging.
The technology event will happen in Las Vegas from March 17 to 19, 2026. The 3-day event will bring together innovative professionals to discuss the future of EV.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is supply chain sustainability software?
▼
Supply chain sustainability software helps organizations assess, monitor, and improve the ESG performance of their suppliers through risk scoring, audit management, and compliance tracking for regulations like CSDDD and LkSG.
### How do I assess supplier ESG performance?
▼
Use supplier assessment platforms that combine self-reported questionnaires, third-party data feeds, risk scoring algorithms, and on-site audit management. Leading tools cover human rights, environmental impact, and governance criteria.
### What regulations require supply chain due diligence?
▼
Key regulations include the EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD), EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR), German Supply Chain Act (LkSG), and UK Modern Slavery Act. Requirements vary by company size and jurisdiction.
### How much does supply chain sustainability software cost?
▼
Pricing ranges from $10,000/year for basic supplier screening to $50,000-$200,000/year for enterprise solutions with multi-tier mapping, automated risk assessment, and full compliance reporting capabilities.
### How do I manage Scope 3 supply chain emissions?
▼
Start with supplier engagement tools that collect primary emissions data, use spend-based models for initial estimates, then transition to activity-based calculations. Platforms with supplier portals and automated data collection reduce the burden significantly.

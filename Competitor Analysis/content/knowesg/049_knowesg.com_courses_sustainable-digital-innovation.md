## Course Details
In the digital age, two of the most significant challenges facing businesses today are rapid digitalization and the urgent need for sustainability . The "Sustainable Digital Innovation" course addresses this critical intersection, offering a unique perspective on how aligning business strategy with sustainability goals can unlock significant new opportunities. This course is ideal for professionals seeking to enhance their leadership skills and integrate digital sustainability corporate training within their organizations. Through online learning, participants will gain a profound understanding of how digital technologies can drive sustainable practices and create value. This self-paced course is designed for individuals looking to initiate or advance a career focused on sustainable development and digital innovation, appealing to a wide range of professionals from aspiring managers to seasoned business leaders.
Create a clear outlook of the sustainable contextual environment of businesses.
Learn effective approaches and techniques in order to solve various sustainability issues.
Understand how to use the digital technologies to facilitate sustainable development within the society and business.
Identify current and developing case studies of it-enabled sustainable-solutions.
This course is divided into five detailed modules, and it can offer a detailed look into green digital innovation.
Module 1: Sustainable Development—How does it relate to a business?
Module 2: Integrating Sustainability into Business Management
Module 3: Aligning Sustainability in industrial digitalization:
Module 4: Sustainable Development—A Source for Innovation
Module 5: Prepare for transformation
The training is developed on a self paced basis which will enable students going through the course by their own time. It utilises fascinating suggestions of some well-known luminaries and the largest business executives including Bjorn Stigson (previously President of WBCSD), Elaine Weidman Grunewald (Sustainability executive and founder of AI Sustainability Center), Henrik Henriksson (previously the President and CEO of Scania), and Annika Ramskold (Vice President Sustainability at Vattenfall).
Upon completion of the "Sustainable Digital Innovation" course, participants will be equipped with a unique blend of knowledge and skills highly sought after in today's market.
Enhanced Expertise: Develop a robust understanding of how to merge digital technologies with sustainable practices for business advantage.
Career Advancement: Position yourself as a leader capable of driving digital sustainability corporate training and initiatives within any organization.
Problem-Solving Skills: Gain practical methods and tools to identify and solve complex sustainability challenges using digital solutions.
Strategic Thinking: Learn to align business strategy with sustainability goals, creating new opportunities and fostering innovation in the digital age.
Industry Insights: Benefit from the wisdom and experience of prominent business leaders and experts in the field of sustainability and digitalization.
Pathway to Further Learning: The course serves as an excellent foundation for those interested in the advanced live professional course, "Sustainable Digital Innovation."
After passing each of the five modules of this digital sustainability corporate training course, Coursera will present you with a Certificate of Completion. This certification introduces the participants to the most essential knowledge and abilities to promote digital innovation and implement sustainable practices as well as develop viable approaches to carbon emissions reduction and resource optimization. This certificate will look great in your resume and business networking sites such as LinkedIn to show your achievements in reducing the digital presence and environmental effect of a business.
Issuing Institute: Coursera (in collaboration with the most established course providers)
Recognition: Endorsed by business leaders, this certificate demonstrates your ability to develop tailored action plans that enable lasting benefits. Employers seeking specialists in sustainable practices and digitalization will value your expertise in applying course‑based innovations to real‑world challenges.

### EIT Digital
EIT Digital EIT Digital is a European education and innovation organisation with a mission to foster digital technology innovation and entrepreneurial talent for economic growth and quality of life. By linking education, research, and business, EIT Digital empowers digital top talent for the future. EIT Digital provides online and face-to-face Innovation and Entrepreneurship education to raise quality, increase diversity and availability of the top-level content provided by 20 leading technical universities around Europe. The universities deliver a unique blend of the best of technical excellence and entrepreneurial skills and mindset to digital engineers and entrepreneurs at all stages of their careers. The academic partners support Coursera’s bold vision to enable anyone, anywhere, to transform their lives by accessing the world’s best learning experience. This means that EIT Digital gradually shares parts of its entrepreneurial and academic education programmes to demonstrate its excellence and make it accessible to a much wider audience. EIT Digital’s online education portfolio can be used as part of blended education settings, in both Master's and Doctorate programmes, and for professionals as a way to update their knowledge.

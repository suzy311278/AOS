## Frequently Asked Questions
### Which ESG certification is best?
The CFA ESG Certificate is widely recognized in finance, while Harvard's Sustainable Investing program is ideal for executives. Choose based on your industry and career goals.
### How much do ESG courses cost?
ESG course prices range from free (UN programs) to $3,500+ (Harvard, Yale). Most professional certifications cost between $500-2,000.
### How long does it take to get ESG certified?
Most ESG certifications take 4-8 weeks of part-time study (5-10 hours/week). The CFA ESG Certificate requires about 100 hours total study time.
### Are ESG certifications worth it?
Yes, ESG certifications are increasingly valuable. 87% of companies now require ESG knowledge for sustainability roles, and certified professionals earn 15-20% more on average.
### What's the difference between ESG and CSR certification?
ESG certifications focus on measurable environmental, social, and governance metrics used by investors. CSR certifications cover broader corporate responsibility and community engagement.

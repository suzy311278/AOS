## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is social impact software?
▼
Social impact software helps organizations measure and manage the "S" in ESG—diversity and inclusion metrics, pay equity analysis, human rights due diligence, employee engagement, community investment, and stakeholder relations.
### How do I measure diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI)?
▼
DEI software tracks workforce demographics, pay gaps, promotion rates, and inclusion sentiment across protected characteristics. It identifies representation gaps, measures belonging scores, and benchmarks against industry peers and targets.
### What is pay equity analysis software?
▼
Pay equity tools use statistical analysis to identify unexplained pay gaps based on gender, ethnicity, and other factors after controlling for legitimate variables like role, experience, and location. They provide remediation recommendations and compliance documentation.
### How do whistleblowing systems support ESG compliance?
▼
Whistleblowing platforms enable confidential reporting of misconduct—fraud, discrimination, safety violations, environmental breaches. They support EU Whistleblower Directive compliance with case management, investigation workflows, and audit trails.
### What is board governance software?
▼
Board governance platforms digitize board operations—meeting management, document sharing, voting, and compliance tracking. They support ESG oversight with dashboards for sustainability KPIs, risk monitoring, and director skill matrices.

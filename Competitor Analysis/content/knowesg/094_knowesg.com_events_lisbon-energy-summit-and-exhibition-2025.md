## _Europe's Leading Energy Transition Event Happening in Lisbon (March 24 to 25, 2026)_
**Event Description**
The Lisbon Energy Summit & Exhibition will take place from 24 to 25 March 2026 in Lisbon, Portugal.
The two-day event will bring together innovators and thought leaders to discuss various sustainability topics such as net zero, decarbonisation, and greener energy systems, as well as share insights on how to transition the E.U.’s energy system to one based on renewables and in line with the REPowerEU initiatives.

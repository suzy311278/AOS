## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is biodiversity monitoring software?
▼
Biodiversity monitoring software tracks nature-related impacts and dependencies using satellite imagery, eDNA analysis, and ecological modeling. It helps organizations assess biodiversity risks, set nature-positive targets, and report under TNFD framework.
### What is TNFD and how do I comply?
▼
The Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures (TNFD) provides a framework for reporting nature-related risks and opportunities. Software helps with the LEAP approach (Locate, Evaluate, Assess, Prepare) and generates TNFD-aligned disclosures.
### How do I measure biodiversity impact?
▼
Biodiversity impact is measured using metrics like Mean Species Abundance (MSA), biodiversity footprint, ecosystem integrity indices, and species richness. Tools combine geospatial analysis with lifecycle assessment to quantify impacts across operations and supply chains.
### What is a nature-positive strategy?
▼
A nature-positive strategy aims to halt and reverse biodiversity loss by 2030. It includes measuring baseline impacts, setting science-based targets for nature, implementing conservation actions, and investing in nature-based solutions and restoration projects.
### How does water stewardship relate to biodiversity?
▼
Water stewardship addresses water quality, quantity, and ecosystem health in watersheds where organizations operate. Software tracks water withdrawals, pollution, and basin-level risks while supporting collaboration with local stakeholders on shared water challenges.

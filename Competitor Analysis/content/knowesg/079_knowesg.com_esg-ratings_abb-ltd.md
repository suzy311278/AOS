ESG & Sustainability Profile
ABB Ltd is a global manufacturer and distributor of electrification, industrial automation, robotics, and motion technologies for utilities, industry, transportation, and infrastructure. IBM and ABB Ltd have a strategic partnership. ABB Ltd is based in Zurich, Switzerland, and was founded in 1883.

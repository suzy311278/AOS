## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is energy management software?
▼
Energy management software monitors, analyzes, and optimizes energy consumption across facilities. It tracks real-time usage, identifies inefficiencies, manages renewable energy procurement, and helps achieve ISO 50001 certification and net zero targets.
### How much does energy management software cost?
▼
Energy management systems range from $2,000-$50,000/year depending on facility size and features. Basic monitoring starts at $1,500/year, while enterprise platforms with AI optimization and multi-site management cost $30,000-$100,000/year.
### What is a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA)?
▼
A PPA is a long-term contract to buy electricity directly from a renewable energy generator at a fixed price. Corporate PPAs help companies secure clean energy, lock in costs, and meet renewable energy targets for 10-20 years.
### How do I track Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs)?
▼
REC tracking software manages the procurement, retirement, and reporting of renewable energy certificates. It ensures compliance with RE100 commitments, Scope 2 accounting requirements, and prevents double-counting across markets.
### What is ISO 50001 and how does software help?
▼
ISO 50001 is the international standard for energy management systems. Software helps by automating energy data collection, tracking Key Performance Indicators (EnPIs), managing audits, and generating documentation required for certification and surveillance audits.

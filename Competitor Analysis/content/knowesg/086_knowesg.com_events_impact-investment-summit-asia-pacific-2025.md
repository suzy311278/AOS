## _Impact Investment Summit Asia Pacific: Sustainable Investing Event in NSW, Australia (March 25-26, 2026)_
**Event Description**
Active institutional investors, Australia’s biggest foundations and family offices, advisors eager to learn, government looking to partner, and companies and funds seeking capital to scale.
The largest cohort of the Summit audience consists of investors seeking to learn more about social and environmental challenges and explore opportunities to invest in companies, funds, and projects that provide solutions. By presenting experts, advisers, and dealmakers and showcasing deals, the Summit achieves its mission of scaling investment for positive impact.

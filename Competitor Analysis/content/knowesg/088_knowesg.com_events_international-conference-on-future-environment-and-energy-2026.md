## _Future Environment and Energy Transition Event Happening in Fukuoka, Japan (March 6-8, 2026)_
**Event Description**
The International Conference on Future Environment and Energy will take place from March 6 to 8, 2026, in Fukuoka, Japan.
The three-day event is sponsored by Sophia University, Japan, and will bring together international scholars and academicians to present novel and fundamental advances in the fields of future environment and energy. It will also include distinguished keynote speeches and three conference days full of papers, abstracts, and more.

Offered by: University of Colorado Boulder
### Level
Mixed
### Duration

### Certification

### Price
Free
## Course Details
In this course, learners will identify the types of actions that we can pursue to address climate change. These actions fall into two broad categories: 1) mitigation, which refers to efforts to reduce greenhouse gas emissions or enhance carbon sinks, and 2) adaptation, which refers to our preparations for climate impacts. We will explore the technologies, programs, and policies related to both mitigation and adaptation. Learners should leave the course with an improved ability to identify and evaluate climate actions undertaken by communities, governments, and businesses. This course is part of the Exploring Our Responses to Climate Change Specialization 
When you enrol in this course, you'll also be enrolled in this Specialization.

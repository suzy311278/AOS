# The Age of Sustainable Development
Offered by: Columbia University
### Level
Beginner, Intermediate
### Duration
4 - 7 months
### Certification
Certificate Available
### Price
Free
## Course Details
The Age of Sustainable Development" gives students an understanding of the key challenges and pathways to sustainable development - that is, economic development that is also socially inclusive and environmentally sustainable. Learn about sustainable development from prominent professors and understand the inequalities between countries along with learning about what can be done to achieve a sustainable global food supply and a sustainable future.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are consumer sustainability apps?
▼
Consumer sustainability apps help individuals track their environmental footprint, make eco-friendly purchasing decisions, discover sustainable products, and change daily habits through gamification and personalized recommendations.
### How do personal carbon tracking apps work?
▼
Personal carbon trackers calculate your footprint from transportation, diet, shopping, and home energy use. They connect to bank accounts, travel apps, and smart home devices to automate tracking and suggest reduction actions.
### What is eco-label verification technology?
▼
Eco-label verification apps scan product barcodes to verify sustainability claims, certifications, and ingredient sourcing. They decode greenwashing by checking against databases of verified certifications and supply chain data.
### How do brands use B2B2C sustainability platforms?
▼
B2B2C platforms enable brands to engage customers on sustainability—carbon offsetting at checkout, product footprint transparency, recycling programs, and loyalty rewards for sustainable choices that drive both impact and customer retention.
### What behavior change features drive sustainable habits?
▼
Effective features include gamification (points, badges, leaderboards), social challenges, personalized goals, progress visualization, and nudges tied to daily routines. The best apps make sustainable choices the easy default.

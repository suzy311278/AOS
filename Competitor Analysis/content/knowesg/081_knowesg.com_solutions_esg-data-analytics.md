## What is ESG Data & Analytics Software?
ESG data and analytics platforms aggregate, normalize, and analyze sustainability information from multiple sources to provide actionable insights for investment decisions, risk management, and competitive intelligence. These solutions combine company-disclosed data with alternative data sources including satellite imagery, news sentiment, regulatory filings, and supply chain intelligence. Advanced platforms use AI and machine learning to identify patterns, predict controversies, and benchmark performance against peers.
### Who Needs ESG Data & Analytics Software?
  * Asset managers integrating ESG factors into investment decisions
  * Risk managers monitoring portfolio exposure to ESG controversies
  * Corporate sustainability teams benchmarking against competitors
  * Procurement teams assessing supplier ESG performance
  * Research analysts requiring comprehensive ESG intelligence


## Key Features to Look For
### Multi-Source Data Aggregation
Combine company disclosures, regulatory filings, news feeds, and alternative data (satellite, geospatial, social) into unified ESG profiles. Cover 50,000+ public and private companies globally.
### AI-Powered Controversy Monitoring
Real-time scanning of 100,000+ news sources in 30+ languages. NLP-based severity scoring and automatic company attribution. Alerts for material ESG incidents within hours.
### Custom ESG Scoring
Build proprietary ESG scores using your own methodology and weightings. Combine third-party ratings with internal assessments and alternative data signals.
### Peer Benchmarking
Compare companies against sector peers, regional averages, and best-in-class performers. Identify leaders and laggards across specific ESG dimensions.
### Alternative Data Integration
Incorporate satellite data (emissions, deforestation), geospatial analysis (water stress, climate risk), and web-scraped signals (job postings, product recalls).
### API & Data Feeds
Enterprise APIs for integration into investment platforms, risk systems, and internal tools. Bulk data feeds for quantitative analysis and model development.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is ESG data analytics software?
▼
ESG data analytics platforms aggregate and analyze sustainability data from multiple sources—company disclosures, news feeds, satellite imagery, and proprietary datasets—to provide risk intelligence, benchmarking, and investment insights.
### How much does ESG data cost?
▼
ESG data subscriptions range from $5,000-$500,000/year depending on coverage and depth. Basic company ESG scores start at $3,000/year, while comprehensive datasets with controversy monitoring and alternative data can exceed $300,000/year.
### What is ESG controversy monitoring?
▼
Controversy monitoring uses AI and NLP to scan news, social media, and regulatory filings for ESG-related incidents—environmental violations, labor disputes, governance scandals—providing real-time alerts and severity scoring for risk management.
### How do ESG ratings differ between providers?
▼
ESG ratings vary significantly due to different methodologies, data sources, and weightings. MSCI, Sustainalytics, and ISS use distinct frameworks, often producing divergent scores for the same company. Multi-provider analysis is recommended.
### What alternative data is used for ESG analysis?
▼
Alternative ESG data includes satellite imagery (deforestation, emissions), geospatial data (water stress), web scraping (job postings for DEI), supply chain mapping, and sentiment analysis from social media and news sources.

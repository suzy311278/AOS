## What is Carbon & Climate Solutions Software?
Carbon accounting software measures, tracks, and reports greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions across Scope 1 (direct emissions from owned sources), Scope 2 (indirect emissions from purchased energy), and Scope 3 (all other indirect emissions in the value chain). These platforms help organizations establish emissions baselines, set science-based targets (SBTi), model decarbonization pathways, and track progress toward net zero commitments. Advanced solutions include product carbon footprinting, supplier engagement tools, and carbon credit management.
### Who Needs Carbon & Climate Solutions Software?
  * Companies setting science-based targets (SBTi) for emissions reduction
  * Organizations with net zero or carbon neutrality commitments
  * Manufacturers with complex Scope 3 supply chain emissions
  * Companies subject to SEC climate rules or CSRD carbon disclosure
  * Businesses participating in carbon markets or offset programs


## Key Features to Look For
### GHG Protocol-Compliant Accounting
Calculate emissions following GHG Protocol Corporate Standard and Scope 3 Guidance. Automated emission factor databases (DEFRA, EPA, ecoinvent) ensure accurate, auditable calculations.
### Scope 3 Value Chain Tracking
Comprehensive Scope 3 coverage across all 15 categories. Supplier engagement portals collect primary data while spend-based models provide estimates where primary data is unavailable.
### Science-Based Target Setting
Model near-term and long-term targets aligned with 1.5°C pathways. SBTi validation support and progress tracking dashboards with automated variance analysis.
### Decarbonization Scenario Modeling
What-if analysis for reduction initiatives—renewable energy procurement, efficiency investments, supplier switches. Cost-benefit modeling helps prioritize interventions.
### Product Carbon Footprinting
Calculate cradle-to-gate and cradle-to-grave carbon footprints at SKU level. Support for Environmental Product Declarations (EPD) and customer carbon data requests.
### Carbon Credit & Offset Management
Track purchased and retired carbon credits. Registry integrations (Verra, Gold Standard) ensure transparency and prevent double-counting.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is carbon accounting software?
▼
Carbon accounting software measures, tracks, and reports greenhouse gas emissions across Scope 1 (direct), Scope 2 (energy), and Scope 3 (value chain) categories, helping organizations set science-based targets and plan decarbonization strategies.
### How do I track Scope 3 emissions?
▼
Scope 3 tracking requires supplier engagement tools, spend-based estimation models, and activity-based calculations. Leading platforms automate data collection from suppliers and apply emission factors aligned with the GHG Protocol.
### What is the difference between carbon accounting and ESG reporting?
▼
Carbon accounting focuses specifically on measuring and reducing greenhouse gas emissions, while ESG reporting covers broader environmental, social, and governance metrics including diversity, labor practices, and board governance.
### How much does carbon accounting software cost?
▼
Pricing typically ranges from $5,000/year for small businesses to $30,000-$100,000/year for enterprise solutions. Key cost drivers include number of emission sources, supply chain complexity, and reporting requirements.
### How long does it take to implement carbon management software?
▼
Basic carbon accounting setup takes 2-6 weeks, while comprehensive implementations covering Scope 1-3 with supplier integrations typically require 2-4 months depending on data availability and organizational complexity.

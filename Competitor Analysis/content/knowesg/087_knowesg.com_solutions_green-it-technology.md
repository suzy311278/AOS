## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is green IT software?
▼
Green IT software helps organizations measure and reduce the environmental footprint of technology operations—cloud computing emissions, data center energy use, software carbon intensity, and IT asset lifecycle impacts.
### How do I measure cloud carbon emissions?
▼
Cloud carbon tools integrate with AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud to track compute, storage, and networking emissions. They provide workload-level carbon attribution, identify optimization opportunities, and support Scope 3 reporting for technology spend.
### What is software carbon intensity?
▼
Software Carbon Intensity (SCI) measures grams of CO2 equivalent per functional unit of software. The Green Software Foundation standard helps developers build carbon-aware applications by optimizing code efficiency, timing workloads for clean energy, and reducing resource consumption.
### How do I improve data center efficiency?
▼
Data center efficiency is measured by Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE). Software monitors cooling, power distribution, and server utilization to identify waste. Best practices include free cooling, hot/cold aisle containment, and AI-driven workload optimization.
### What is sustainable IT asset disposition (ITAD)?
▼
ITAD software manages the end-of-life of IT equipment—tracking assets, ensuring secure data destruction, maximizing reuse and recycling, and generating compliance certificates. It extends equipment life through refurbishment and supports circular economy goals.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is circular economy software?
▼
Circular economy software helps organizations track material flows, manage waste streams, conduct lifecycle assessments (LCA), and optimize recycling and reuse processes to minimize waste and maximize resource efficiency.
### How much does waste management software cost?
▼
Waste management software typically costs $5,000-$50,000/year depending on features. Basic waste tracking starts at $3,000/year, while comprehensive lifecycle assessment platforms can exceed $100,000/year for enterprise deployments.
### What features should I look for in circular economy tools?
▼
Key features include waste stream tracking, material flow analysis, lifecycle assessment (LCA), recycling rate optimization, Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) reporting, and integration with supply chain systems.
### What is lifecycle assessment (LCA) software?
▼
LCA software analyzes the environmental impact of products throughout their entire lifecycle—from raw material extraction through manufacturing, use, and end-of-life disposal or recycling. It quantifies carbon footprint, water usage, and resource depletion.
### How do I comply with EPR regulations?
▼
Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) compliance requires tracking packaging and product volumes, calculating fees, reporting to national registries, and managing take-back schemes. Specialized software automates fee calculations and regulatory submissions across jurisdictions.

###  Is the SDG Academy a university? 
No. The SDG Academy is a non-profit educational initiative that creates free massive open online courses (MOOCs), educational videos, webinars, and other educational resources on sustainable development and makes them available as a global public good. 
If you are interested in pursuing a degree in sustainable development, consider enrolling in an [Online Master’s program](https://sdgacademy.org/online-masters-in-sustainable-development/) with one of our university partners. The programs offer rigorous, comprehensive curricula focusing on the pressing issues of our time, taught by leading academics. The programs are fully online and use the power of technology to combine live teaching with the SDG Academy’s online materials.
The SDG Academy is also the host of the [Global Association of Master’s in Development Practice (MDP) Programs](https://sdgacademy.org/global-mdp-program/). The MDP is a two-year graduate-level curriculum in development practice offered at roughly 30 universities worldwide. Consult this list of  to see where MDP programs are available.
###  What educational resources does the SDG Academy offer? 
The SDG Academy is committed to providing both content and community to support education for sustainable development in post-secondary institutions and through lifelong learning. 
**Content:**
  * **(MOOCs)** – To date, the SDG Academy has created 50+ MOOCs on sustainable development and the SDGs. They are available for free and can be accessed on edX.org, one of the world’s largest online learning platforms.
  * – A searchable repository of more than 2,000 educational videos on sustainable development and the SDGs. It contains lecture videos and case studies from our MOOCs, exclusive video series, recordings of webinars and expert Q&As, and videos curated from select partners. Users can search for content by SDG, subject, lecturer, language, series, or keyword. The SDG Academy Library is a free resource available to everyone.
  * **Expert Webinars** – We host expert webinars and Q&As on topics relevant to sustainable development, including pedagogy and curriculum development around the SDGs. Follow the SDG Academy on social media or 
  * [**_Book Club with Jeffrey Sachs_ podcast**](https://sdgacademy.org/book-club-with-jeffrey-sachs/) – Hosted by Professor Jeffrey Sachs, the eminent thought-leader in economics and sustainable development, the podcast features conversations with renowned authors about their groundbreaking work in sustainability, economics, history, social justice, and more.


**Community:**
  * [**Online Master’s Programs in Sustainable Development**](https://sdgacademy.org/online-masters-in-sustainable-development/) – The SDG Academy partners with accredited universities around the world to offer innovative Online Master’s Programs in Sustainable Development. 
  * [**Global Association of Master’s in Development Practice Programs**](https://sdgacademy.org/global-mdp-program/) – Since 2019, the SDG Academy has hosted the Secretariat of the Global Association of MDP Programs. The MDP is a two-year graduate-level curriculum in development practice offered at roughly 30 universities worldwide. 


###  What is a MOOC? 
A massive open online course (MOOC) is comprehensive, educational content provided in a globally accessible, online format. Many MOOCs consist of lecture-style videos, assessments, readings, and discussions. 
To date, the SDG Academy has created 50+ MOOCs on the SDGs and related topics in sustainable development. Our MOOCs are hosted on edX.org, one of the world’s largest online learning platforms. [Browse](https://sdgacademy.org/courses) our course catalog or  on edX.
###  Is SDG Academy content available only in English? Can I take a MOOC in my language? 
At this time, most of our content has been developed in English. Some course videos include subtitles in additional languages. Please see [individual course pages](https://sdgacademy.org/courses) for the subtitle languages available for each MOOC.
Subtitles in English and additional languages are also available for most videos in the .
###  How much does it cost to take an SDG Academy MOOC? 
You can enroll in any SDG Academy course on edX _for free_ via the Audit Track. As an Audit Learner, you will have access to all course videos, readings, discussions, activities, and ungraded assessments. 
Learners who want to pursue an  will need to pay a fee to upgrade to the Verified Track. Verified Learners who pass the course will receive an edX Verified Certificate signed by the course instructor(s). Please consult the grading policy for each individual course.
EdX offers financial assistance for learners who cannot afford to upgrade.  to learn more about the edX Financial Assistance program. 
In addition to the edX Verified Certificate, several of our courses offer free Certificates of Completion, generously issued by our partners:
  * _[Nature-based Solutions for Disaster and Climate Resilience](https://sdgacademy.org/course/nature-based-solutions-for-disaster-and-climate-resilience/)_ , issued by UNEP
  * _[Living Heritage and Sustainable Development](https://sdgacademy.org/course/living-heritage-and-sustainable-development/)_ , issued by UNESCO
  * _[Ecosystem-based Adaptation: Working with nature to adapt to a changing climate](https://sdgacademy.org/course/ecosystem-based-adaptation/)_ , issued by IISD
  * _[Equity and Inclusion in Education](https://sdgacademy.org/course/equity-and-inclusion-in-education/)_ , issued by UNESCO


###  Who is eligible to take an SDG Academy MOOC? What are the application requirements or prerequisites? 
Anyone with internet access can take an SDG Academy course. There are no age restrictions, applications, or prerequisites required. However, some courses are more advanced than others. Please refer to individual courses for details.

###  How do I get a certificate? / I haven’t received my certificate. 
To earn an edX Verified Certificate in an SDG Academy MOOC on edX, you must upgrade to the Verified Track and verify your identity. Once verified, if you successfully complete the course, you should be able to find a link to your certificate in the course under the “Progress” tab. ###  Can I earn academic credit for completing an SDG Academy MOOC? 
Not directly. The SDG Academy is not an accredited educational institution and does not grant academic credit. 
However, our _Master’s Pathway in Foundations of Sustainable Development_ on edX provides a flexible pathway to accreditation at University College Dublin. [Click here](https://sdgacademy.org/masters-pathway-in-foundations-of-sustainable-development/) to learn more.
Additionally, some universities have allowed their students to complete an SDG Academy MOOC as part of an “independent study” or other academic program. Consult your institution to see if this is an option for you. 
Please note that the SDG Academy does not provide proof of completion or credit hours.

## Overview
**The SDG Academy is pleased to partner with the Ban Ki-moon Centre to promote this course!** _Becoming Global Citizens for a Sustainable Society_ can be accessed through 
_
The Ban Ki-moon Centre presents the “Becoming Global Citizens for a Sustainable Society” Online Course. The course aims to empower all learners to connect their passion, interests, and skills to become impactful global citizens.
The self-paced Online Course consists of 5 modules and is full of interactive assignments, videos and plenty of new information and reflection spaces for learners to explore climate change and their role in tackling it. Let’s dive into this exciting topic, where knowledge, inspiration, and opportunity await!

## This course is for
**Those who see themselves as global citizens** as well as who aspire to assume active roles in bringing meaningful changes to oneself and to the society they are in.

## Course logistics and requirements
This course is self-paced. Please note that this course is not owned or facilitated by the SDG Academy. Any questions can be sent to the Ban Ki-moon Centre team. 
## syllabus
Module 1: Global Citizenship and the SDGs
Module 2: Global Citizenship in a Challenging World
Module 3: Act to Change: Global Citizenship for Transformation
Module 4: Meet the Global Citizens Around the World
Module 5: Plan for Action: Becoming Active Global Citizens

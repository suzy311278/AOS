#### The Course
  

Looking to lead the transition to a **low-carbon economy** and play a strategic role in shaping **climate accountability** in the UAE? Explore the legal and regulatory heart of the Emirates’ climate transformation with this cutting-edge programme, designed to equip you with the skills to interpret, implement, and lead on **compliance** with **Federal Decree-Law No. 11 of 2024**. From emissions reporting and reduction strategies to adaptation planning, internal governance, and enforcement preparedness, **this course provides a comprehensive framework** for navigating the UAE’s new climate law across sectors.
You’ll gain practical insights into aligning your organisation’s operations with legal obligations, avoiding penalties, and **turning compliance into a strategic asset**. Through case studies, scenario planning, and simulated audits, you’ll learn how to build climate governance systems that stand up to scrutiny, satisfy regulators, and enhance stakeholder trust. Whether you are advising clients, managing operations, or setting policy, this diploma will empower you to move beyond minimum compliance, **positioning you to lead confidently** in a region where environmental accountability is firmly embedded in law and a competitive differentiator.  

#### What you will learn
When we designed the **Diploma in UAE Climate Law** , our aim was to create a practical, strategic learning experience for professionals navigating the UAE’s evolving climate governance landscape. Every module is carefully structured to build your understanding of the legal foundations, regulatory requirements, and compliance strategies set out in **Federal Decree-Law No. 11 of 2024 on the Reduction of Climate Change Effects**.
You’ll begin with a grounding in the scope and structure of the law, then progress into sector-specific obligations, emissions reporting, adaptation planning, internal governance, enforcement mechanisms, and the strategic benefits of early compliance. Each lesson connects legal interpretation with real-world application, through case studies, scenario planning, and regulatory simulations, regardless of your background in law, policy, or sustainability.
By the end of the course, you’ll have a solid command of the UAE climate law and the skills to confidently implement it across your organization or advisory work. You'll be equipped not just to meet legal requirements, but to turn compliance into a competitive advantage, strengthening governance, preparing for inspections, and positioning your business as a climate leader in the Emirates.

#### The Course
**The Diploma in CBAM Regulation** equips professionals with the knowledge and operational capability to navigate one of the most consequential trade and climate regulations ever introduced by the European Union. The program moves beyond introductory explanations to provide a structured, practice-oriented understanding of the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism as a binding trade, customs, and financial compliance regime.
The diploma begins by establishing the legal and policy foundations of CBAM and its relationship with the EU Emissions Trading System, before examining the full lifecycle of CBAM compliance, from scope determination and CN code classification to embedded emissions calculation, data governance, certificate surrender, enforcement, and inspections. It explores CBAM from multiple perspectives, including EU importers, non-EU exporters, procurement teams, finance functions, and sustainability and risk professionals.
Participants gain in-depth insight into how CBAM interacts with international carbon pricing mechanisms, supply chains, contracts, and global trade rules, including WTO considerations. The program also addresses compliance risk management, audit readiness, penalties, and how CBAM reshapes market access, sourcing strategies, and competitiveness in carbon-adjusted trade.
Designed for professionals responsible for compliance, trade operations, sustainability strategy, finance, and advisory roles, this diploma enables learners to move from conceptual understanding to practical readiness. By the end of the program, participants will be able to interpret CBAM requirements confidently, manage data and supplier risks, support inspections, and position their organisations strategically in a carbon-constrained EU market.  

#### What you will learn
The **Diploma in CBAM Regulation** is designed to give participants a decisive advantage in a trade environment where carbon-adjusted costs, regulatory scrutiny, and data credibility increasingly determine access to the EU market. Organisations across sectors now face binding CBAM obligations that affect customs operations, supplier relationships, pricing structures, and financial exposure, making CBAM readiness a core business capability rather than a peripheral compliance task.
This program focuses not only on understanding the CBAM legal framework, but on applying it in real operational and commercial contexts. Participants will learn how to identify CBAM-covered goods, interpret system boundaries, manage embedded emissions data, coordinate importer–exporter responsibilities, and navigate the transition from reporting to certificate purchase and surrender. The diploma also builds practical competence in managing compliance risks, responding to enforcement and inspections, and integrating CBAM considerations into contracts, procurement, and market strategy.
Learners will develop the confidence to engage effectively with suppliers, customers, auditors, and regulators, to explain CBAM impacts clearly to internal decision-makers, and to translate complex regulatory and emissions data into defensible, business-relevant actions. With its applied focus and trade-oriented perspective, the diploma enables professionals to move beyond reactive compliance and position CBAM as a lever for risk control, cost predictability, and long-term competitiveness in the EU market.

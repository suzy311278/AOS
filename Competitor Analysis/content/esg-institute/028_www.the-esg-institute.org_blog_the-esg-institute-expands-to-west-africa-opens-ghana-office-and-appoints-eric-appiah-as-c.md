# The ESG Institute Expands to West Africa: Opens Ghana Office and Appoints Eric Appiah as Country Head
Mar 25
Written By [The ESG Institute](https://www.the-esg-institute.org/blog?author=6495d387bc6f46275ebd3a5d)
![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/642e883205cdec341bcebf38/fed209d7-d06d-4a35-9e1e-19f4c77aaaf3/Eric+Appiah+The+ESG+Institute+Ghana.jpg)
**Accra, Ghana – 28th March 2025** – The ESG Institute is proud to announce the opening of our new office in **Ghana** , reinforcing our commitment to driving sustainable development across Africa. This move follows the incorporation of **Appiah Information Technology Systems (AITS)** (
The new office will be led by **Eric Appiah** , who has been appointed **Country Head for Ghana**. Eric is an award-winning technology and sustainability expert, known for bridging the gap between digital transformation and environmental responsibility. He was recently recognized as **Tech Personality of the Year in Ghana** for his contributions to IT innovation and his role in advancing sustainable tech solutions.
Eric’s background includes an MSc in **Environmental Sustainability and Management** and a BSc in **Computer Science** , complemented by ongoing postgraduate studies in **Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics**. He holds certifications from institutions such as **Harvard Business School** and the **University of London** , and has led sustainability-focused IT strategies at organizations like **Global Volunteers Corps** and **AITS**.
A frequent speaker on ESG and technology, Eric has delivered workshops on **AI for Business Growth** , **Cybersecurity** , and **Green IT** at regional and international conferences—including engagements with the **US Embassy in Ghana** , **Africa Digital Skills Conference** , and **CXO Portals UK**.
**“Joining The ESG Institute as Country Head is an incredible opportunity to help shape the future of sustainable development in Ghana. I believe that technology, when aligned with ESG principles, can drive lasting impact—empowering businesses, protecting our environment, and uplifting communities. I’m excited to lead this movement and collaborate with changemakers across the region.”**  
—  _Eric Appiah, Country Head – Ghana, The ESG Institute_
“Eric Appiah brings a unique ability to align technology and sustainability,” said **Jaime Amoedo** , Executive Director of The ESG Institute. “His leadership will accelerate our mission to support Ghanaian businesses and institutions in integrating ESG principles into their operations.”
The Ghana office will serve as a hub for ESG advisory, training, digital sustainability solutions, and regional collaboration—furthering The ESG Institute’s vision of a more sustainable and resilient global economy.
**For businesses seeking guidance on how to navigate today’s evolving sustainability landscape, The ESG Institute is your trusted partner.** We offer expert insights, training, regulatory compliance strategies, and tailored ESG solutions designed to help your organization thrive in a world that demands greater transparency and accountability.
Whether you’re just beginning your sustainability journey or looking to enhance existing efforts, our global network of professionals is ready to support you every step of the way.
[**Reach out to The ESG Institute today**](https://www.the-esg-institute.org/contact)**—because building a sustainable future starts with bold, informed action.**

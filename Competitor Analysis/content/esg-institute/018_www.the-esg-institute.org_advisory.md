## **Advisory Services**
```
At The ESG Institute, we provide end to end ESG advisory services designed to support organisations at any stage of their sustainability journey. Our global team of specialists works closely with clients to evaluate their current position, identify opportunities, and implement practical, future ready ESG solutions.   
```
```
Our advisory work follows a structured 10-step roadmap that ensures clarity, alignment, and measurable impact:
```

![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/642e883205cdec341bcebf38/f2293edd-ad2e-486d-b877-975e38b054fc/The+ESG+Institute+Roadmap.png)
  1. **ESG Readiness Assessment** - We begin by evaluating the organisation’s current ESG maturity, performance, and strategic gaps. This baseline assessment helps clients understand where they stand and what is required to advance.
  2. **Stakeholder Mapping and Materiality** - We identify the stakeholders who influence or are affected by the business and prioritise ESG topics based on impact, relevance, and regulatory expectations.
  3. **Risk and Opportunity Mapping** - We analyse climate, environmental, social, and governance related risks alongside emerging opportunities to strengthen strategic resilience.
  4. **ESG Strategy Development** - We help define measurable goals, guiding policies, governance structures, and a multi year roadmap aligned with global standards and frameworks.
  5. **Regulatory Alignment -** We ensure clients comply with CSRD, ESRS, TCFD, IFRS Sustainability Standards, and other national or sector specific regulatory requirements.
  6. **Carbon Footprinting and GHG Accounting -** We measure Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions using recognised methodologies and certified tools, laying the foundation for credible reduction pathways.
  7. **Supply Chain ESG Monitoring -** We enhance transparency and resilience across supplier networks through tailored assessments, ESG risk screening, due-diligence processes, and monitoring systems.
  8. **ESG Reporting and Disclosures -** We prepare high quality, audit ready sustainability reports using trusted platforms and in line with global reporting frameworks.
  9. **Solution Matching and Implementation -** We connect clients with vetted partners, technologies, and service providers from our global network to help them execute their ESG plans effectively.
  10. **ESG Training and Capacity Building -** We equip internal teams with the skills needed to embed ESG into day to day operations through customised training, workshops, and certified programs.

## **Educational Accreditation**
### **Not-For-Profit (NGO/Charity/Association)**
Complete the questionnaire below to begin the accreditation process for your organisation. It takes just a few minutes to tell us about you.
![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/642e883205cdec341bcebf38/633e3077-adc8-4563-9978-ba8992df45d2/The+ESG+Institute+Accredited+Training+Institution.png)
### **What Happens Next**
Once we review your submission (usually within **2–4 business days**), we will:
  * If approved, send you a secure payment link to finalise your accreditation fee.
  * If we need more information, reach out to you with clarifying questions.


Thank you for applying, our goal is to make the process smooth, transparent, and efficient. We look forward to welcoming you!

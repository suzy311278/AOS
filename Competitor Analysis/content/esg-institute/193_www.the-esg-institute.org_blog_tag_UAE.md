#  [ The UAE Climate Law Is Here. Is Your Business Ready? ](https://www.the-esg-institute.org/blog/the-uae-climate-law-is-here-is-your-business-ready)
The UAE’s new climate law, Federal Decree-Law No. 11 of 2024, mandates businesses to measure, report, and reduce emissions by May 2025. With significant penalties for non-compliance, the law marks a shift from voluntary action to legal obligation, offering risks and opportunities for companies ready to lead in sustainability.

#  [ Championing Sustainability: The ESG Institute's Strategic Partnership with the National Advisor Bureau ](https://www.the-esg-institute.org/blog/the-esg-institute-partners-with-national-advisor-bureau)
In a pivotal move for global sustainability, The ESG Institute has joined hands with the renowned National Advisor Bureau. This collaboration promises to harness both parties' strengths, focusing on advancing sustainable practices in businesses worldwide. Together, they aim to shape a future where ESG principles are deeply integrated into every business facet, championing a more sustainable and equitable tomorrow.

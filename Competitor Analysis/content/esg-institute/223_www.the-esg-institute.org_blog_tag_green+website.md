#  [ The ESG Institute and Bioscore Collaborate to Empower Digital Sustainability ](https://www.the-esg-institute.org/blog/esg-institute-and-bioscore-collaborate-to-empower-digital-sustainability)
Our new collaboration with Bioscore integrates real-time digital carbon tracking into businesses’ core operations. By offering actionable insights and AI-driven recommendations, this partnership empowers companies to reduce their digital carbon footprint, comply with sustainability regulations, enhance brand reputation, and lead the way toward more sustainable and responsible digital practices.

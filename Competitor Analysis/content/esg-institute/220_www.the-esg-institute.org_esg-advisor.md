## **Senior Sustainability Consultant**
**Role Overview:**
The ESG Institute is seeking experienced professionals **from around the world** to join our **Panel of Freelance Senior Sustainability Consultants and Trainers**. These individuals will bring valuable expertise and insights to support a wide range of projects and learning programs, advancing our mission to build a more sustainable and responsible world.
Consultants and Trainers may be engaged on a project-by-project basis. You may choose to contribute as a strategic advisor, a training facilitator, or both, depending on your background, interests, and availability.
**Key Responsibilities:**
  * **Subject Matter Expertise:** Provide expert consulting on sustainability and ESG-related matters in your specific area of focus, supporting the planning, analysis, and execution of varied projects.
  * **Strategic Input:** Advise on the development and refinement of sustainability strategies, frameworks, and policies, ensuring alignment with leading standards and best practices.
  * **Collaborative Engagement:** Partner with our internal teams and external clients to assess and address ESG risks, opportunities, and performance improvements.
  * **Training & Facilitation: **Design and/or deliver high-quality sustainability and ESG training sessions, from introductory webinars to advanced corporate workshops.
  * **Knowledge Sharing:** Support the development of learning materials and resources, and contribute expert insights to enhance the effectiveness of our education programs.
  * **Thought Leadership:** Represent The ESG Institute in conferences, events, or publications, helping to drive global conversations around ESG and sustainable development.


**Qualifications:**
  * Proven track record as a consultant, trainer, or subject matter expert in ESG and/or sustainability.
  * In-depth knowledge of ESG frameworks and reporting standards (e.g. CSRD, ESRS, GRI, TCFD, ISSB...).
  * Strong communication skills and the ability to translate complex concepts into actionable strategies or engaging training content.
  * Demonstrated experience in thought leadership, client advisory, or professional development.
  * Collaborative spirit and a flexible, solution-oriented approach.


**Why Join Us:**
As a Senior Sustainability Consultant, you’ll have the opportunity to:
  * Shape the sustainability practices of businesses across industries.
  * Contribute to impactful education and advisory work.
  * Collaborate with a passionate, global network of sustainability leaders.
  * Work flexibly, according to your expertise and availability.


We are committed to **diversity and inclusion** and warmly welcome applicants from all backgrounds.
**Additional Details:**
Compensation: Project-based. Compensation will vary depending on the complexity and duration of each project.
Location: This is a remote position. Advisors may be located anywhere in the world, although some of the projects may require in-person meetings.
Commitment: We value your expertise and understand that many of our advisors may have other professional commitments. We are flexible in terms of engagement and only request your involvement in projects that align with your expertise and availability.
**About The ESG Institute**
At The ESG Institute, we are shaping the future of business by empowering organizations to embed sustainability and ESG principles at their core. Through our global network of advisors, training programs, strategic projects, and thought leadership, we help businesses navigate complexity, drive impact, and thrive in a changing world.
Our culture is built on collaboration, purpose, and continuous learning, giving you the platform, flexibility, and freedom to contribute meaningfully. Whether you're joining us for a single project or building a lasting relationship, your experience at The ESG Institute is designed to be both professionally rewarding and personally inspiring.
If you believe your skills, experience, and passion align with the role, we would love to hear from you.

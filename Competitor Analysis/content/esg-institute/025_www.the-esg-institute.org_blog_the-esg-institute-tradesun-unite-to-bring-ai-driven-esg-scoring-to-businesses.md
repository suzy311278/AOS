# The ESG Institute & TradeSun Unite to Bring AI-Driven ESG Scoring to Businesses
Jan 13
Written By [The ESG Institute](https://www.the-esg-institute.org/blog?author=6495d387bc6f46275ebd3a5d)
![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/642e883205cdec341bcebf38/f08af300-18f2-48b2-98ec-439ccadc699f/The+ESG+Institute+%26+TradeSun+Unite+to+Bring+AI-Driven+ESG+Scoring+to+Businesses.png)
The ESG Institute is proud to announce a strategic partnership with **TradeSun, Inc**., the innovator behind **CoriolisESG** , the world's first automated ESG scoring solution. This partnership is set to revolutionize the trade finance and sustainability sectors by combining expert ESG consulting services with advanced digital solutions. 
Under this agreement, The ESG Institute and TradeSun will unite their expertise to deliver top-tier ESG technology and consulting services to organizations globally. This collaboration is aimed at harnessing the best of both organizations to elevate sustainability and operational efficiency across various industries, setting a new benchmark for responsible business practices.
"Joining forces with TradeSun marks a pivotal moment for The ESG Institute," stated **Jaime Amoedo** , Executive Director and co-founder. "TradeSun's prowess in leveraging CoriolisESG to dissect and score business operations against global sustainability benchmarks is exactly what we need to enrich our sustainability integration services. This collaboration enhances our toolkit, empowering us to offer businesses nuanced, data-driven insights that drive genuine sustainable transformation." 
CoriolisESG by TradeSun has demonstrated its value through significant collaborations with over 70 organizations, including leading financial institutions like **Santander** , **Lloyds Banking Group** , and **First Abu Dhabi Bank** , as part of the Kosmos Working Group. This initiative has been instrumental in developing standardized ESG scoring criteria and methodologies, fostering transparency and accountability across supply chains and corporate activities. The platform's comprehensive approach covers **over 460 million companies globally** , offering insights into compliance with the UN Sustainable Development Goals, the EU Taxonomy, and various other regulatory frameworks. 
![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/642e883205cdec341bcebf38/c687bd05-a0bc-4b20-b93e-0fa656a75171/Coriolis+TradeSun+The+ESG+Institute.png)
"I am thrilled to be creating this alliance with The ESG institute," stated **Nigel Hook** , Founder & CEO of TradeSun. “CorolisESG was developed to provide unparalleled insight into the ESG practices and associated risks of global businesses”. The power of the CoriolisESG’s reporting coupled with The ESG Institute’s industry expertise means that businesses of all sizes can become more operationally efficient, sustainable and responsible. Extending the reach of CoriolisESG’s insights through this partnership allows more companies and their supply chains to orient around sustainability-embedded business practices." 
This collaboration promises to empower businesses with the tools and knowledge necessary to navigate the complexities of ESG compliance and sustainable growth, combining The ESG Institute's consulting prowess with TradeSun's technological innovation. 
For further details on this transformative partnership and the services offered, please visit www.the-esg-institute.org and www.tradesun.com.
**About The ESG Institute**
The ESG Institute, leveraging a global network of over 500 experts, is a **leading authority in integrating Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles into business strategies** to drive meaningful change. With a focus on innovation and practicality, The ESG Institute provides a comprehensive suite of services, including ESG training, consulting, and certification programs. By equipping organizations with the knowledge and tools to meet evolving sustainability standards, The ESG Institute enables businesses to not only achieve compliance but also create long-term value. Its mission is to transform businesses into agents of positive change, fostering a more sustainable, equitable, and resilient global economy.
**About TradeSun, Inc.**
TradeSun is a **pioneering force in trade digitization** , known for its innovative solutions in compliance and automated ESG scoring. Its product range, including Real-Time Compliance and the groundbreaking CoriolisESG, is revolutionizing how trade and supply chains manage ESG and financial crime risks. TradeSun's AI-driven trade finance platform is recognized for its ability to minimize risks and drive growth for banks and corporates by utilizing advanced technologies. CoriolisESG, notable for being the first of its kind, offers a robust, user-friendly ESG scoring system, enhancing business intelligence by aligning with global sustainability frameworks.

## **Educational Accreditation**
**The ESG Institute** offers accreditation services to institutions, educators, and organisations delivering ESG and sustainability education. This accreditation serves as a mark of **excellence, integrity, and credibility** , ensuring that educational providers and learning programs meet global standards and contribute meaningfully to a more sustainable future.

### **What is an Accreditation?**
Accreditation is the formal recognition that a course or educational provider meets **high-quality, globally aligned, and impact-driven standards** in sustainability education. It helps:
  * Build **trust and credibility** with learners, employers, and institutional partners
  * Ensure educational content is aligned with key **international ESG frameworks** , such as:
    * Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
    * EU Taxonomy for Sustainable Activities
    * Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)
    * European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS)
    * Global Reporting Initiative (GRI)
    * Task Force on Climate-Related Financial Disclosures (TCFD)
    * International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB)
    * Science-Based Targets initiative (SBTi)
    * UN Global Compact Principles
    * OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises
    * IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards
  * Promote **continuous improvement** in program design, delivery, and learner outcomes
  * Safeguard learners from **greenwashing** and misinformation in the sustainability learning space

### **The Accreditation Process**
  1. **Application:** Providers submit an online application with supporting documents (syllabus, trainer profiles, assessment strategy, etc.)
  2. **Initial Review:** Our team screens for completeness and provides preliminary feedback.
  3. **Evaluation:** Independent ESG experts conduct a detailed review, with optional site visits or digital walkthroughs.
  4. **Accreditation Decision:** We issue accreditation for 1 year, with clear feedback and a unique ID and badge.
  5. **Monitoring:** Annual light-touch reviews and optional refresher trainings are required to maintain accreditation status.


### **Who Can Be Accredited?**
  * Training Institutions & Academies
  * Universities & Business Schools
  * NGOs and Nonprofits
  * Public and Private Sector Learning Providers
  * Individual Trainers and Coaches


### **Why Should You Get Accredited?**
  1. **Enhanced Credibility:** Demonstrates your commitment to delivering high-quality sustainability education.
  2. **Global Alignment:** Ensures your content matches leading sustainability frameworks.
  3. **Learner Trust:** Builds confidence with students, partners, and employers.
  4. **Market Visibility:** Accredited providers will be listed on our global registry and can display The ESG Institute’s Accreditation Badge.
  5. **Competitive Edge:** Distinguishes your courses in an increasingly crowded ESG training landscape.

# Apple's Carbon Neutrality Milestone: Raising the ESG Standard with the Apple Watch Lineup
Sep 13
Written By [Jaime Amoedo](https://www.the-esg-institute.org/blog?author=6495d39d82ae3248fe49c1ca)
![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/642e883205cdec341bcebf38/22bf57f6-84c1-4882-9ecd-d593cf80dbe8/New+Apple+The+ESG+Institute.png)
Apple's recent announcement about its new Apple Watch lineup is a remarkable testament to the company's relentless pursuit of a sustainable and carbon-neutral future. In a groundbreaking move, Apple has introduced its **first-ever carbon-neutral products** , a significant step towards its ambitious 2030 climate goal. These products have achieved outstanding reductions in carbon emissions, showcasing Apple's unwavering commitment to environmental responsibility and innovation.
Apple's carbon-neutral Apple Watch lineup is the result of extensive efforts across the company and its global supply chain. The company has embraced clean energy sources like wind and solar power, **leading to a dramatic reduction of over 75% in product emissions for each carbon-neutral Apple Watch**. This achievement not only sets a high standard for the industry but also underscores Apple's determination to make every product carbon neutral by the end of the decade, including the entire supply chain and the lifetime use of each device.
Beyond carbon neutrality, Apple has taken several commendable steps to minimize its environmental impact. The company has **eliminated the use of leather across all its product lines, opting for sustainable alternatives**. The introduction of entirely fiber-based packaging for the new Apple Watch lineup further demonstrates Apple's commitment to eco-friendly practices. These actions highlight Apple's dedication to sustainability throughout the entire lifecycle of its products.
One particularly innovative feature introduced by Apple is the Grid Forecast tool in the Home app. This tool informs users about the availability of cleaner energy sources in their power grid, enabling them to make informed decisions about electricity usage. By promoting responsible electricity consumption, Apple empowers users to reduce their carbon footprint.
What sets Apple apart is its rigorous approach to product decarbonization. The company prioritizes the reduction of greenhouse gas emissions across the product lifecycle, emphasizing electricity, materials, and transportation. **Only after significant emissions reductions are achieved does Apple consider using high-quality carbon credits from nature-based projects to offset any remaining emissions**. This approach reflects Apple's genuine commitment to environmental responsibility.
![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/642e883205cdec341bcebf38/c8185bf1-9570-4669-ba4e-b06b1a25ea22/Screenshot+2023-09-13+at+18.28.08.png)
Each carbon-neutral Apple Watch model adheres to strict criteria, **including 100% clean electricity for manufacturing and product use, 30% recycled or renewable materials by weight, and 50% of shipping without air transportation**. These criteria result in an impressive reduction of at least 75% in product emissions for each model, and they are certified as carbon neutral by SCS Global Services, a respected environmental standards and certification organization.
Apple's journey towards sustainability began over a decade ago, and the company now supports over 15 gigawatts of clean energy worldwide, equivalent to powering over 5 million American homes. This commitment extends not only to Apple's operations but also to its suppliers, with over **300 global suppliers committing to 100% renewable electricity for all Apple production by the end of this decade**.
In conclusion, Apple's carbon-neutral Apple Watch lineup is a testament to the company's dedication to sustainability and innovation, setting a remarkable example for businesses worldwide. 
While this achievement is a significant milestone, it's important to remember that the path to a sustainable future is marked by both triumphs and challenges. Apple's commitment to reducing its environmental footprint is undoubtedly commendable, but it's just the beginning of a long and complex journey. 
Achieving carbon neutrality across the entire product range and global supply chain by 2030 is an audacious goal, and it will require continued innovation, collaboration, and sustained efforts. 
Moreover, Apple's commitment to a **90 percent reduction in emissions by 2050** underscores the ongoing nature of this journey. It's a reminder that the road to sustainability is marked by milestones, but it's also riddled with obstacles, which we must collectively overcome to safeguard our planet. 
Apple's journey towards a carbon-neutral future, responsible material sourcing, and cleaner transportation methods inspire hope for a brighter, more sustainable world for all. It's a reminder that the work ahead is substantial, but with determination and shared commitment, we can overcome the challenges and pave the way for a more sustainable future.

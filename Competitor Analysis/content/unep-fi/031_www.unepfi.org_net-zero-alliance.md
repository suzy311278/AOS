# UN-convened Net-Zero Asset Owner Alliance
Global asset owners committed to decarbonizing their investment portfolios and achieving net-zero emissions by 2050
Net-Zero Asset Owner Alliance is a signatory-led group of institutional investors committed to individually transitioning their own investment portfolios to net-zero greenhouse gas emissions by 2050 – consistent with a maximum temperature rise of 1.5°C.
NZAOA asset owners are the finance industry’s first to set intermediate targets, which include CO2 reduction ranges for 2025 (22 – 32%) and for 2030 (40% – 60%).

### Key information
###### Credible targets 
Using NZAOA’s Target-Setting Protocol, 79 signatories (with a combined AuM of USD 9.4 trillion) have now [set](https://www.unepfi.org/themes/demonstrating-1-5c-aligned-decarbonisation-the-fourth-progress-report/) Paris-aligned 2025 decarbonization targets.
###### Portfolio emission reduction
NZAOA’s [latest data](https://www.unepfi.org/themes/demonstrating-1-5c-aligned-decarbonisation-the-fourth-progress-report/) shows that all signatory cohorts (based on the base year for reporting) have recorded reductions in absolute financed emissions of at least 6 per cent annually, in line with the IPCC’s 1.5ºC pathways.
###### Investing in climate solutions
Starting with climate solution investments of US$87 billion in 2020, NZAOA signatories invested a total of $743 billion as at end of 2023. 
###### Signatories
Launched at the UN Secretary-General’s Climate Ambition Summit in September 2019 with 12 members, NZAOA now has 87 signatories across 19 countries with US$9.2 trillion in assets under management. 
###### Robust methodologies
NZAOA has built and expanded science-based methodologies for setting climate targets. The [fourth edition of its Target-Setting Protocol](https://www.unepfi.org/industries/target-setting-protocol-fourth-edition/), released in 2024, covers the entirety of signatories’ private assets and almost the entirety of signatories’ portfolios.

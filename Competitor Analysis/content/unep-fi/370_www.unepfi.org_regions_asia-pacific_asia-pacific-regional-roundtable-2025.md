###### **English**
**The UN Environment Programme Finance Initiative (UNEP FI) hosted the Regional Roundtable Asia Pacific from June 19–20, 2025, in Suzhou, China. The event, open to UNEP FI members and non-members, played a pivotal role in advancing sustainable finance across the Asia Pacific region and accelerated the transition to a net-zero and just economy.**
As part of UNEP FI’s biennial series of five major regional events on sustainable finance in 2025, the Roundtable convened leading finance professionals, policymakers, and sustainability experts to explore innovative strategies and share best practices in sustainable finance. The agenda featured key discussions on net-zero banking and insurance, climate and transition finance, financing clean energy, just transition, and ESG data solutions, among others. This dynamic platform supported stakeholders in addressing critical challenges and collectively drove the sustainable finance agenda forward.

**联合国环境规划署金融倡议（UNEP FI）已于2025年6月19日至20日在中国苏州举办亚太地区区域圆桌会议。 此次会议面向UNEP FI会员及非会员开放，在推动亚太地区可持续金融发展方面发挥了关键作用，加速了向净零和公平经济的转型。**
作为联合国环境署金融倡议五个主要区域之一，2025 年的会议是关于可持续金融双年度系列活动的一部分。圆桌会议召集了区域内主要的金融专业人士、政策制定者和可持续发展专家，探讨了可持续金融的创新战略，并分享了最佳实践。会议议程重点讨论了净零银行和保险、气候和转型融资、清洁能源融资、公正转型和环境、社会和公司治理数据解决方案等议题。通过系列会议活动，UNEPFI支持了利益相关者应对重大挑战，共同推动可持续金融议程向前发展。

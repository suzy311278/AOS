# Join NZAOA
### Why join NZAOA?
Institutional investors play a key role in helping the world limit global warming to 1.5°C. NZAOA signatories represent a significant portion of the financial sector and are delivering on a bold commitment to transition their investment portfolios to net-zero greenhouse gas emissions by 2050. NZAOA invites fellow leadership institutions to join this effort.
Asset owners that are considering joining NZAOA can find more information in the following documents.
### The NZAOA's foundational documents
##### [Commitment Document for Participating Asset Owners ](https://www.unepfi.org/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/New-NZAOA-Commitment.pdf "Commitment Document for Participating Asset Owners")
This document spells out the details of the commitment as well as the NZAOA’s process for delisting. Upon submitting a signed commitment, an institution joins the NZAOA.
##### [The NZAOA's Governance ](https://www.unepfi.org/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/2025.11_AOA-revised-Governance-Document-1-1.pdf "The NZAOA's Governance")
This document describes the structure of the NZAOA as well as the roles and responsibilities of its various stakeholders.
##### [The NZAOA's FAQ ](https://www.unepfi.org/net-zero-alliance/about/frequently-asked-questions-net-zero-asset-owner-alliance/ "The NZAOA's FAQ")
The frequently asked questions on commitment implementation and participation in the NZAOA are addressed here.

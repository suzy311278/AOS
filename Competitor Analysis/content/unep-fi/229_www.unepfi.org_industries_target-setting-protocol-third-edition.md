## Target-Setting Protocol Third Edition
The Net-Zero Asset Owner Alliance’s Target-Setting Protocol is a foundational document that created a framework for reporting and delivery on short-term climate targets. Since the launch of the [Inaugural Target Setting Protocol](https://www.unepfi.org/publications/inaugural-2025-target-setting-protocol/), the document – and thus the expectations for members – have become more comprehensive and robust (see also the [Target Setting Protocol Second Edition](https://www.unepfi.org/publications/target-setting-protocol-second-edition/)).

# UNEP FI Global Roundtable 2026
23-24 June 2026 | London, United Kingdom and Online
UNEP FI is bringing the 19th edition of the Global Roundtable on Sustainable Finance (GRT) to London **on 23 and 24 June 2026** , to host one of the UN’s largest sustainable finance-focused events alongside London Climate Action Week. This year’s theme, ‘**From Risk to Resilience: Financing the Future** ,’ reflects the growing need across the financial industry to respond to global challenges with vision and leadership for a resilient future.

The biennial UNEP FI Global Roundtable (GRT), held [in collaboration with the **Green Finance Institute**](https://www.unepfi.org/grt2026/#gfi) in 2026, is the premier forum shaping the sustainable finance agenda worldwide. For over three decades, this UN platform has brought together the most influential voices across finance, policy, science and sustainability to accelerate the transformation of the financial system. It provides a unique platform where financial institutions, regulators, supervisors, academics and civil society engage in constructive dialogue on aligning finance with sustainable development.

##### UNEP FI and the Green Finance Institute join forces
The UNEP FI Global Roundtable 2026 will be held in collaboration with the 
The GFI supports governments and financial institutions around the world as they move from sustainability ambition to delivery, with a focus on designing investable and scalable solutions. Operating in Europe, Latin America, Africa, and South East Asia, the GFI’s market‑driven financial and policy experts combine local insight with global market expertise. Through its Transactions to Transitions work, GFI helps align policy objectives with the financial structures and products that enable private capital to deploy at scale — ensuring sustainability outcomes can be achieved in ways that are compatible with institutional mandates and return requirements.
Developed to deliver financial transactions at scale, this approach turns ambition into action by coordinating efforts across policy design, institutional reform, pipeline development, and transaction execution. This execution‑focused model complements UNEP FI’s leadership on frameworks and principles by helping translate them into real‑world investment pathways.
##### 23 June 2026 | High-level plenaries | In-person and online 
UNEP FI Global Roundtable will host high-level plenaries on 23 June 2026. Held under the theme, “From Risk to Resilience: Financing the Future”, the event will host over 500 in-person attendees, and many more online, to highlight the critical role of the finance industry in navigating risk and mobilising finance to build more resilient systems and economies. Past speakers have included Christine Lagarde, Amina Mohammed, Inger Andersen, Kristalina Georgieva, Mark Carney, Christiana Figueres, Al Gore, finance sector CEOs, and more. 
  * **Date and time:** 23 June 2026 | 10:00 – 16:00 BST

  * **UNEP FI members:** If you are a UNEP FI member, please visit the [UNEP FI Members’ Area](https://www.unepfi.org/grt2026/members/) or read the newsletter for priority registration.


##### 24 June 2026 | Engagement Forums | UNEP FI members and partners only
UNEP FI will host 2 interactive roundtables convening up to 150 members, partners sustainability experts and practitioners to explore how resilience can be strengthened across financial systems, economies and societies in the face of climate change, nature loss and sustainability-driven risks.
  * **10:30 – 12:30 BST:** UNEP FI Resilience in Action Forum
  * **13:30 – 15:30 BST:** UNEP FI Real Economy Engagement Forum


**Please note:** These forums are invite-only for UNEP FI members and partners and will not be livestreamed. If you are a UNEP FI member, please visit the [UNEP FI Members’ Area](https://www.unepfi.org/grt2026/members/) or read the newsletter for details

# Revenues for Nature community of practice
A joint initiative from the Green Finance Institute Hive, UNDP Biodiversity Finance Initiative and UNEP Finance Initiative
### Nature needs us to work together
Revenues for Nature (R4N) is a global project that aims to contribute to the implementation of Target 19 of the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (GBF), to mobilize USD 200 Billion per year for biodiversity, by mobilizing private sector finance at scale for nature restoration and conservation.
The Revenues for Nature community of practice brings together a wide range of stakeholders, including banks, insurers, investors, the private sector, governments and local communities, to come up with innovative solutions for the implementation of Target 19. It fosters partnerships at the local, national, regional and global level to connect nature finance strategies from the private sector with National Biodiversity Strategies and Action Plans (NBSAPs) led by governments.
The community of practice aims to foster a collaborative environment where participants across sectors and geographies can share knowledge, exchange ideas, and explore successful financing models; enabling financial priorities to be aligned with biodiversity goals to unlock new investment opportunities that drive sustainable development. Together, this community can create impactful change helping to ensure a nature-positive and sustainable future for all.
### Join us
The community of practice is open to all public or private actors relevant to financing NBSAPs—including asset owners, investors, insurers, development banks, corporations and government representatives—as well as enabling partners such as NGOs and academia.
It aims to collect and mobilize relevant expertise on the challenges and opportunities presented by Target 19. By helping to align the core operations of financial institutions and corporate actors with biodiversity objectives, the community of practice contributes to bridging the financing gaps in NBSAPs, in collaboration with governments.
Participants will receive regular updates on the R4N project through a monthly newsletter and have the opportunity to join regular webinars. Recordings of previous webinars and details on how to register for upcoming webinars will be included below. Additional events may also be planned depending on demand.
Please note that there is no fee to participate in the community. The community is a joint initiative from Green Finance Institute Hive, UNDP Biodiversity Finance Initiative and UNEP Finance Initiative. Activities are independent of UNEP FI membership activities.
### Webinars
###### Public-private finance flowing into GBF Target 19 – trends and innovation
This webinar builds on key insights from experiences with governments and financial institutions, as well as the Revenues for Nature guidebooks. It provides practical frameworks and case studies on financing mechanisms that bridge public and private finance.
###### Biodiversity finance: exploring public and private finance opportunities for NBSAPs
This webinar supports CBD Parties to better understand the possible application of public and private finance to their NBSAP revision, update and implementation. BIOFIN and UNEP FI provide an overview of the partnership and its goals.
###### Nature Bonds: Catalyzing Investment for Biodiversity
This webinar explores the transformative potential of nature bonds as innovative instruments fostering public-private partnerships. It highlights how these bonds can support CBD Parties in revising or updating their NBSAPs ahead of COP16.
###### Revenues, scalability and replicability for nature outcomes: 5 lessons for policymakers and investors
Drawing on emerging insights from models supported under Revenues for Nature and global case studies, the discussion will examine practical approaches already taking root and share early lessons on aligning ecological outcomes, social benefits, and investor returns.

## Regional Roundtable Latin America & Caribbean 2025 (Members-only)
São Paulo, Brazil
1-3 April 2025
  * About
  * Agenda

  * Speakers


**The UN Environment Programme Finance Initiative (UNEP FI) hosted its Regional Roundtable on Sustainable Finance for Latin America and the Caribbean from 1–3 April 2025 in São Paulo, Brazil. This members-only event brought together UNEP FI member banks and insurers to evaluate progress, define next steps, and drive alignment of the region’s financial ecosystem with global sustainability goals and frameworks.**
As part of UNEP FI’s 2025 Regional Roundtable series, this biennial event provided a platform for members to share insights from key global processes, such as COP16 and G20, and prepare for pivotal moments like COP30. Discussions focused on advancing the finance sector’s contributions to sustainable economies, with knowledge exchange on critical issues including climate mitigation and adaptation, sustainable finance policy and regulation, responsible insurance and banking, nature and biodiversity risks, and social impacts.
This event was exclusive to UNEP FI members. **[Read more about the event outcomes here.](https://www.unepfi.org/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/LAC-RRT_FIs-bridging-gaps-LAC-publication.pdf)**

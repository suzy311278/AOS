# Social Issues
Putting people at the centre of financial systems
### About
The finance industry has the power to influence lives, communities, and entire societies. When guided by human rights, inclusion, and equality principles, it becomes a driving force for sustainable transformations that enable people everywhere to thrive. Fairness and justice are essential parts of the solution to the global challenges of climate change, biodiversity loss and pollution. From a business perspective, ethical practices are not only the right thing to do, they deliver a strategic advantage that drives long-term growth, strengthens operational resilience, and future-proofs institutions.
At UNEP FI, we work alongside our members and partners to embed social and human rights considerations into mainstream finance while helping them fulfil the commitments they have made as signatories to the Principles for Responsible Banking and the Principles for Sustainable insurance. We equip financial institutions to respond more effectively to emerging risks and opportunities and support them in channeling resources into businesses and projects that enhance financial health and inclusion, support decent work, and accelerate a just transition that leaves no one behind.

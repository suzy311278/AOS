### Media Coverage
##### [Coverage of the Alliance in 2025 ](https://www.unepfi.org/net-zero-alliance/media/media-coverage-of-the-net-zero-asset-owner-alliance-in-2025/ "Coverage of the Alliance in 2025")
Read the most recent media coverage on the Alliance here. 
##### [Coverage of the Alliance in 2024 ](https://www.unepfi.org/net-zero-alliance/media/media-coverage-of-the-net-zero-asset-owner-alliance-in-2024/ "Coverage of the Alliance in 2024")
In 2024, the Alliance published its standing Target-Setting Protocol (fourth edition), celebrated its five-year anniversary, and released a discussion paper on on Scope 3 portfolio integration.
##### [Coverage of the Alliance in 2023 ](https://www.unepfi.org/net-zero-alliance/media/media-coverage-of-the-net-zero-asset-owner-alliance-in-2023/ "Coverage of the Alliance in 2023")
In 2023, the Alliance released the third version of its Target Setting Protocol as well as its influential policy paper on “Unlocking Investment in Net Zero”. 
##### [Coverage of the Alliance in 2022 ](https://www.unepfi.org/net-zero-alliance/media/2022-media-coverage-of-the-asset-owner-alliance/ "Coverage of the Alliance in 2022")
In 2022, the Alliance released the Second Edition of the Target Setting Protocol, made a Statement on the Escalating Energy Crisis, and supported the use of the One Earth Climate Model results.

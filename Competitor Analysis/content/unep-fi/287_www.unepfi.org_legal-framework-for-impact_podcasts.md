# Podcasts
A Legal Framework for Impact
##### An introduction to the Freshfields Report
| 04 October 2021 | In this episode, PRI’s Director of Policy Margarita Pirovska interviews the co-author of the ‘Legal Framework for Impact’, David Rouch from Freshfields.   
---|---  
* * *
##### AP2’s Investment Approach
| 13 July 2020 | Hosted by PRI’s Olivia Mooney, Martin Jonasson, General Counsel of AP2, and Elodie Feller, former Investment Lead at UNEP FI discuss what investing for sustainability impact means and why it is important. They also dig deeper with specific examples of investor approaches to managing for impact, how it goes beyond niche impact investing, the evolution in EU benchmarks, and balancing the appropriate level of returns and real-world impact.   
---|---  
* * *
##### A Legal Framework for Impact
| 11 May 2020 | Fiona Reynolds, CEO of PRI, speaks with special guest David Blood, Co-Founder and Senior Partner of Generation Investment Management, about the crucial role of investors in considering the real-world impacts of their investments and how A Legal Framework for Impact project addresses this important issue.

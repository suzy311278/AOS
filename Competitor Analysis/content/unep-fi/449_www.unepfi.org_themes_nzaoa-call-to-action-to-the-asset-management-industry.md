## Serving Asset Owner Clients through Climate Stewardship: A call to action to the asset management industry
[Download the call to action here](https://www.unepfi.org/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/NZAOA-CTA-to-AM-industry.pdf)
This call to action sends a clear message to the asset management industry that “Serving Asset Owner Clients [is only possible] through Climate Stewardship”.
The call to action references the Alliance’s previous resources on asset management engagement, covering topics of [Proxy Voting](https://www.unepfi.org/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/16-Elevating-Climate-Diligence-2.pdf), [Policy Engagement](https://www.unepfi.org/industries/aligning-climate-policy-engagement-with-net-zero-commitments/), [Engagement Best Practices](https://www.unepfi.org/industries/elevating-asset-manager-net-zero-engagement-strategies/), and [Private Markets.](https://www.unepfi.org/industries/the-net-zero-asset-owner-alliance-outlines-its-recommendations-for-asset-managers-in-private-markets/)Going a step further, this call to action sets an expectation for asset managers of net-zero committed clients to pursue integrated and tailored climate strategies across all their functions. 
Four critical implementation principles, which require leadership buy-in, are highlighted specifically: 
  * Focus on addressing the systemic risk of climate change;
  * Support an improved proxy voting landscape (for public equity);
  * Align lobbying activities with stated climate-related commitments; and
  * Make climate engagement more systematic and transparent.

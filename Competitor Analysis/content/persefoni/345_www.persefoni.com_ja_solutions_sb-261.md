## **Climate Risk Reporting Can Seem Complex. SB 261 Doesn’t Have to Be.**
California Senate Bill 261 (SB 261) sets stringent requirements for climate risk disclosure. Understanding whether your business is subject to the law, and what’s required to comply, is the first step.
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/63d986594fe2ae25dd7d16b4/68759b39f023e30660c0c34a_psfi__skyline-grid__en2%201.png)
Who It Impacts  

US entity (public and private)  

doing business in California
Companies with over $500M  

in total annual revenue
Key Deadlines  

January 1, 2026  

First SB 261-compliant report must be published publicly
Biennial updates  

Climate-related financial risk reports must be updated every two years thereafter
What to Report  

A Climate-Related Financial Risk Report consistent with the Task Force on Climate-Related Financial Disclosure (TCFD) or International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB) framework
Explanation of the measures adopted to reduce and adapt to identified risks

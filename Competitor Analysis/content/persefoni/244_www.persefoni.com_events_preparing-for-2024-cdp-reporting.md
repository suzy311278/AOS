Join us for an exclusive fireside chat featuring Paul Dickinson, the Founding Chair of CDP and a member of Persefoni’s Sustainability Advisory Board. This webinar will delve into the critical updates introduced in the 2024 CDP Questionnaire, providing valuable insights on how these changes will impact companies and how to best prepare for this disclosure year.
**Learning objectives:**
  * Gain insights into the significant changes introduced in the 2024 CDP questionnaire and how it compares to previous years.
  * Understand the alignment of CDP reporting with the ISSB S2 Climate Change Standard and the benefits it brings to the reporting process.
  * Explore practical tips and strategies for preparing your CDP response, including the use of technology services partners, and tools available for SMEs.
  * Discover key market trends driving increased CDP disclosures, including the role of supply chain programs and emerging requesting authorities.

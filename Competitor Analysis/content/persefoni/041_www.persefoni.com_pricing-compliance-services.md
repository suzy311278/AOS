**Questions & Answers**

How do I know if my company is subject to California’s SB 253 or SB 261?
![](https://www.persefoni.com/pricing-compliance-services)
Companies that meet certain revenue and business thresholds in California may be required to comply. SB 253 applies to large companies (public and private) with annual revenues over $1 billion that operate in California, while SB 261 applies to companies with annual revenues over $500 million doing business in the state. If you’re unsure, please consult with your legal counsel to assess your organization’s applicability.

How is the SB 253/261 managed reporting service different from using Persefoni’s Advanced platform?
![](https://www.persefoni.com/pricing-compliance-services)
The Advanced platform gives your team direct access to Persefoni’s full suite of carbon accounting and disclosure tools. With managed services, our experts do the work for you, handling data uploads, reviews, and disclosure drafting, so your team has minimal lift. Both approaches use the same underlying technology, but the managed service is ideal if you want a turnkey, low-effort compliance solution. If you need a broader reporting solution beyond California compliance, advanced scope 3 capabilities, and decarbonization planning tools, the Advanced platform is a better fit for your needs.

Can I start with managed services and later transition to the Advanced platform? 
![](https://www.persefoni.com/pricing-compliance-services)
Absolutely. Many customers will opt to use managed services to quickly meet immediate compliance deadlines, then move to the Advanced platform for ongoing, in-house reporting or to access our full suite of robust carbon accounting and sustainability management capabilities. Because both are built on Persefoni’s system, your data and disclosures transfer seamlessly.

Do the managed services include access to the Persefoni platform? 
![](https://www.persefoni.com/pricing-compliance-services)
Yes. You’ll have limited access to Persefoni’s platform for visibility, data management, and records. Managed services simply layer on expert delivery so you can outsource the day-to-day work.

Can Persefoni support auditor assurance? 
![](https://www.persefoni.com/pricing-compliance-services)
Yes. Our audit-ready ledger, built-in controls, and expert support are designed to streamline assurance processes.

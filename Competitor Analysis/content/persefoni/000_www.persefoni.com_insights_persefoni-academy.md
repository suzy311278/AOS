# Persefoni Academy
Persefoni Academy is your go-to learning hub for carbon accounting and climate disclosure. Whether you're just getting started or advancing your expertise, our expert-led lessons and practical tools help you navigate sustainability reporting with confidence.
[ Course California Climate Disclosure Compliance This course provides a clear, practical overview of California’s landmark climate disclosure laws—SB 253 and SB 261. Learn what these regulations mean for your business, how to prepare for compliance, and what legal, sustainability, and risk teams need to know to stay ahead. ](https://www.persefoni.com/sign-up/california-climate-disclosure-compliance)
[ Course Carbon Accounting Essentials The business world is in a race to reduce its carbon footprint. But how do we calculate and measure it? This course covers the basics you will need to get started! Intended for those who are just getting started in carbon accounting or need a refresher. ](https://www.persefoni.com/sign-up/carbon-accounting-essentials)
[ Course Decarbonization 101 In a world focused on combatting climate change, businesses must grasp their carbon footprints and embrace reduction strategies. This course equips you with the knowledge, tools, and strategies to navigate corporate decarbonization effectively. ](https://www.persefoni.com/sign-up/decarbonization-101)
[ Course Scope 3 101 This course introduces Scope 3 emissions and explains how organizations collect data, apply calculation methods, and improve value-chain emissions reporting over time. ](https://www.persefoni.com/sign-up/scope-3-101)

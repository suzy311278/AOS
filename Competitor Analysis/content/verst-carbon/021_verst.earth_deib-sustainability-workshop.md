## DEIB - Sustainability Workshop for DEIB in Kenya
### About The Project
From September 2023 to February 2024, Verst Carbon facilitated a comprehensive Sustainability Workshop for DEIB in Kenya, designed to build capacity among corporate professionals on managing and reducing emissions across their value chains. 
This workshop focused on educating participants about Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions and Product Lifecycle Assessment (LCA). The goal was to equip attendees with the knowledge and tools to assess and mitigate their organizations’ environmental impact, ensuring sustainability is integrated into corporate decision-making processes.

### The Solution
![](https://verst.earth/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/image-36-1024x336.png)
The DEIB Sustainability Workshop provided a structured, in-depth training program to address these knowledge gaps. The curriculum was designed to give participants a practical understanding of emissions management and product lifecycle assessment, with a focus on real-world corporate applications.
### Impact Made So Far
The DEIB Sustainability Workshop had a profound impact on the participating companies and professionals, significantly enhancing their capacity to manage environmental sustainability in corporate settings.
By the end of the workshop, participants were not only knowledgeable about emissions and lifecycle assessments but also capable of implementing sustainability initiatives that align with corporate goals and global standards. This capacity-building initiative represents an important step forward in fostering sustainable corporate practices in Kenya and beyond.

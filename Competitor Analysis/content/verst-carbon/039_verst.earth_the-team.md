## Meet The Verst Carbon Team
The team driving Verst Carbon’s mission to drive environmental sustainability and economic growth by advancing Africa’s participation in carbon markets 
Sure is responsible for establishing relationships with investors and securing funding for our carbon projects. He develops and executes innovative financing strategies, advises on fundraising, and supports projects to get investor-ready. He also brings valuable expertise in leveraging emerging technologies in carbon markets. Sure holds a Bachelor of Science in Finance and various certifications in financial markets, data science, and AI.
Antony Sure
## Director, AI & Innovation
Nyangena spearheads our mission to eliminate barriers hindering Africa's participation in carbon markets. He provides strategic alignment and is the vision bearer for the company. Nyangena holds a Bsc. in Actuarial Science, an MSc. In Mathematical Finance, an MSc. in Financial Engineering, and is currently pursuing a PhD. in Blockchain. 
Brian Nyangena
## CEO
Charles heads our carbon projects development team. He leverages his extensive knowledge and expertise on carbon methodologies to ensure that our projects adhere to the highest standards of environmental integrity and carbon accounting. Charles holds a Bsc. in Mathematics, PGD in GHG Accounting and he is currently pursuing an MSc. in Environmental Science.
Charles Waweru
## Director, Carbon Projects Development
Curtis, holding a BSc in Information Technology and AWS certifications, is a DevOps enthusiast experienced in Solutions Architecture, Mobile App Development, and Microservices. He develops scalable, secure systems that enhance business efficiency and manages high-performance cloud applications on AWS and Azure. Passionate about innovation, Curtis brings a unique perspective and aligns his goals with our mission.
Curtis Wilson
## DEVOPS Engineer
Ian Mutai is the technology lead at Verst Carbon. He brings to the team his extensive experience in building successful tech startups. He is tasked with providing strategic and technical guidance to our tech team. Ian Mutai holds Bsc. in Software Engineering and an Msc. in Information Systems and Technology.
Ian Mutai
## CTO
Sandra is a Carbon Market Analyst at Verst Carbon, specializing in the application and analysis of carbon methodologies to support projects in achieving measurable emission reductions. With a strong foundation in Economics and Statistics, she plays a pivotal role in ensuring that projects adhere to the rigorous standards required for carbon credit certification. Sandra's analytical expertise and focus on methodology analysis enable her to drive sustainability initiatives that align with both environmental impact and market needs.
Sandra Kwamboka
## Carbon Markets Associate 
Zecharia Mwangi is a Carbon Associate at Verst Carbon, specializing in carbon methodologies and accounting for CDR and energy projects. With a background in electrical engineering, he brings technical expertise to identifying the best-fit carbon methodologies for various projects, ensuring compliance with carbon standards. Zecharia is skilled in calculating project emission reductions, removals and avoidance, often developing customized calculation tools. He’s dedicated to advancing climate solutions through innovation and sustainability.
Zecharia Mwangi
## Carbon Markets Associate - CDR and Energy
Ziana is a Carbon Markets Associate at Verst Carbon, specializing in AFOLU (Agriculture, Forestry, and Other Land Use) projects. With an MSc in Climate Change Science and Impacts, she focuses on carbon market research and policy analysis. Passionate about empowering communities through sustainable solutions, Ziana works with cross-functional teams to develop impactful carbon reduction strategies, contributing her expertise to climate resilience and sustainable development initiatives.
Ziana Chelimo

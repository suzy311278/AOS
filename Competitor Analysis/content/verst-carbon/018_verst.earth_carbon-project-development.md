## Carbon Project Development
At Verst Carbon, our carbon project development services are designed to catalyze impactful climate action across multiple sectors. We lead the development of our own projects in renewable energy, energy efficiency, waste handling and management, and nature-based solutions, ensuring they adhere to the highest environmental and social standards. Our comprehensive approach encompasses every stage of the project lifecycle, from initial design and feasibility studies to implementation, monitoring, and the ultimate issuance of carbon credits.   
  
We also collaborate closely with project proponents, providing expert guidance and support to navigate the complexities of carbon project development. This includes meticulous project documentation, stakeholder engagement, compliance with regulatory requirements, and rigorous verification processes. By leveraging our deep expertise and extensive network, we help bring innovative carbon projects to fruition, contributing to a sustainable and low-carbon future.

### FCDO
The Uganda Climate Smart Jobs (CSJ) initiative is a 4-year program funded by the UK’s International Climate Fund (ICF) and implemented by Verst Carbon. The program aims to promote climate resilience and sustainable economic growth in Uganda.
[ View Case Study ](https://verst.earth/climate-smart-jobs/)
![NACONEK - Thumbnail](https://verst.earth/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/african-kid-marketplace-1024x683.jpg)
### NACONEK​
The project is an initiative National Council for Nomadic Education in Kenya (NACONEK) and Rockefeller Foundation, World Food Program, GEAPP, SE4ALL, and Verst Carbon aimed at transitioning Kenyan schools from traditional biomass cooking to clean cooking technologies. 
[ View Case Study ](https://verst.earth/mass-transition-to-clean-cooking-energy-for-sustainable-school-feeding-through-carbon-finance/)
![](https://verst.earth/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Slide-1-1024x444.png)
### Mandulis Energy
Mandulis Energy, in partnership with Verst Carbon, is leading a transformative bio-energy project aimed at improving rural livelihoods and enhancing agricultural sustainability across Uganda, Botswana, and South Africa.
[ View Case Study ](https://verst.earth/bio-energy-for-agriculture-rural-livelihoods/)

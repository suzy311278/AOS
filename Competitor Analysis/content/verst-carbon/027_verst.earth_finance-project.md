## Financing Philosophy
Verst Carbon is a one-stop-shop for investors seeking to confidently deploy capital into African carbon projects with high climate and social impact. Investors can rely on Verst Carbon’s on-the-ground expertise and technology-driven approach to source, develop, and monitor a pipeline of robust, impactful, and investable projects across the continent. Verst Carbon is a convener of investors and projects seeking funding, helping to bridge the financing gap and unlock the flow of capital into African carbon projects. Through our finance portal, we empower investors to discover and assess high-quality, verified African carbon projects, while also providing developers with the technical support they need to become investment-ready. By offering a range of investor readiness, deal structuring and risk mitigation mechanisms, Verst Carbon helps to lower barriers to investment and improve the economic viability of projects.

## Why Partner With Verst Carbon?
Access to a Pipeline of High-Quality, Vetted African Carbon Projects 
  1. Verst Carbon’s rigorous project sourcing and screening process ensures that investors have access to a curated pipeline of high-quality African carbon projects that meet the highest standards of environmental integrity, social impact, and financial viability.
  2. Our team of experts conducts thorough due diligence on each project, assessing technical feasibility, market potential, and regulatory compliance, to mitigate investment risks and maximize returns.
  3. By partnering with Verst Carbon, investors can save time and resources in identifying and evaluating potential investment opportunities, while gaining exposure to a diverse range of projects across different sectors and geographies. 


Comprehensive Due Diligence Support and Reduced Transaction Costs 
  1. Verst Carbon’s comprehensive services, including project development support, innovative tools for cost reduction, and digital MRV solutions, help to streamline the investment process and reduce transaction costs for investors. Our team of experts conducts thorough due diligence on each project, assessing technical feasibility, market potential, and regulatory compliance, to mitigate investment risks and maximize returns. 
  2. Our AI-powered tools and automated systems minimize the need for manual due diligence and monitoring, empowering investors with insights to make informed decisions quickly and efficiently. 
  3. By leveraging Verst Carbon’s expertise and infrastructure, investors can focus on their core investment activities while we handle the technical and operational aspects of project development and implementation. 


Ongoing Project Monitoring and Reporting Services 
  1. Verst Carbon provides investors with comprehensive monitoring and reporting services throughout the lifecycle of their investments, ensuring transparency and accountability.
  2. Our digital MRV solutions collect and analyze project data in real-time, providing investors with timely and accurate information on project performance, credit issuance, and environmental and social impact.
  3. We also prepare regular investor reports and host investor briefings to keep our partners informed of key developments and milestones, facilitating effective communication and decision-making. 


Expertise in Structuring and Negotiating Financing Agreements 
  1. Verst Carbon’s team of experienced finance professionals provides investors with the expertise needed to structure and negotiate complex financing agreements, such as equity investments, debt facilities, and forward finance contracts.
  2. We work closely with investors to understand their investment objectives, risk appetite, and return expectations, and tailor financing structures that align with their specific needs.
  3. Our deep knowledge of the African carbon market and extensive network of project developers, financial institutions, and legal advisors enable us to secure favorable terms and mitigate potential risks for our investment partners. 


Potential for attractive Returns on investment and Positive Environmental and Social Impact 
  1. Investing in African carbon projects through Verst Carbon offers the potential for attractive financial returns, with many projects generating stable, long-term cash flows from the sale of carbon credits and other sustainable products.   
  

  2. In addition to financial returns, investors can also generate significant positive environmental and social impact, contributing to climate change mitigation, biodiversity conservation, and sustainable development in Africa.   
  

  3. By partnering with Verst Carbon, investors can align their investment strategies with their sustainability objectives and demonstrate their commitment to responsible investment practices.

## Why Raise Finance with Verst Carbon?
Comprehensive financing options 
We offer a wide range of financing options including equity, debt, forward finance, and concessional finance, allowing you to choose the most suitable funding for your carbon project based on its unique characteristics, risk profile, and stage of development. 
Expert guidance and support 
Our team of experienced professionals provides end-to-end support throughout the financing process. We work closely with you to assess your project’s financing needs, develop a compelling and bankable project proposal, identify and engage with potential investors and lenders, structure and negotiate favorable deal terms, and provide ongoing monitoring and reporting. 
Extensive investor and lender network 
We have built an extensive global network of impact investors, private equity firms, development finance institutions, corporate buyers, carbon funds, and other financiers who are actively seeking to invest in high-quality carbon projects. We leverage this network to connect you with aligned investors and lenders, increasing your chances of securing financing. 
Robust financial modeling and risk assessment 
Our team conducts thorough financial modeling and risk assessment to determine your project’s financing needs, expected cash flows, and risk profile. This analysis helps structure financing transactions that align with your project’s specific requirements and risk mitigation strategies. 
Favourable deal terms and risk allocation 
Our expertise in structuring and negotiating financing transactions helps you secure favorable terms for your carbon project. We work to optimize key deal terms such as interest rates, repayment schedules, and collateral requirements for debt finance; ownership and control provisions for equity finance; and pricing, volume, and delivery terms for forward finance. 
Increased visibility and credibility 
By raising financing through Verst Carbon, you benefit from our platform’s strong reputation and credibility in the carbon finance ecosystem. This increased visibility can help you attract additional investors and partners, unlock new opportunities, and scale your project’s impact.

## Our Process
![Step 1: Project Onboarding](https://verst.earth/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/vciconf2.png)
###  Step 1: Project Onboarding 
Initial Submission of your carbon project details through our online application system. Provide us with an overview of your project, its carbon credit potential, and your funding needs. This should include information about the project type (e.g., forestry, renewable energy, energy efficiency), location, expected emission reductions, and the stage of project development (e.g., concept, feasibility study, implementation). 
![Step 2: Preliminary Screening](https://verst.earth/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/vciconf3.png)
###  Step 2: Preliminary Screening 
Our team will conduct a Preliminary Assessment to ensure that your project meets the minimum criteria for carbon credit projects and is in line with international standards, such as the Verified Carbon Standard (VCS), Gold Standard, Puro, Plan Vivo, the Clean Development Mechanism (CDM), and Article 6 frameworks. We will also assess the project’s potential to generate high-quality, verifiable carbon credits and its alignment with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). 
![Step 3: Comprehensive Evaluation and Technical Assistance ](https://verst.earth/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/vciconf4.png)
###  Step 3: Comprehensive Evaluation and Technical Assistance 
A Thorough Due Diligence evaluation will be carried out, during which we will also provide technical assistance services to prepare your project for investors by addressing any gaps. (The steps may vary depending on the type of financing being sought). 
![step 4. Feedback Loop](https://verst.earth/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/vciconf5.png)
###  step 4. Feedback Loop 
Detailed Feedback will be provided to enhance your project’s strengths and address any gaps identified during the due diligence process. Our team will work closely with you to refine your project documentation, financial projections, and risk mitigation strategies, positioning you better for potential investors in the carbon credit market. 
![Step 5: Investor Engagement](https://verst.earth/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/vciconf1.png)
###  Step 5: Investor Engagement 
Upon successful due diligence, we will facilitate Investor Matchmaking, connecting your project with our extensive network of financiers interested in supporting carbon credit initiatives. This may include impact investors, institutional investors, carbon funds, and corporate buyers seeking to offset their emissions. We will assist in preparing targeted investment proposals and arranging investor meetings or online presentations. 
![Step 6: Advisory for Final Steps](https://verst.earth/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/vciconf6.png)
###  Step 6: Advisory for Final Steps 
Detailed Feedback will be provided to enhance your project’s strengths and address any gaps identified during the due diligence process. Our team will work closely with you to refine your project documentation, financial projections, and risk mitigation strategies, positioning you better for potential investors in the carbon credit market.

# Certified Climate Resilient Officer (CRO)
The Certified Climate Resilient Officer (CRO) Course aims to equip professionals with all the necessary skills and knowledge in order to integrate Climate Risk into Strategic Planning. Additionally, it provides insight on the potential financial risks of climate change for Corporations and Cities. The course will also help you understand the methods for assessing these risks and the importance of Climate Risk Disclosure in sustainability reporting, complying with the latest carbon-related standards and frameworks, such as ISO 14091, WBCSD, GHG Protocol or SBTi. Through the Certified Climate Resilient Officer Course you will explore case studies in this field from IKEA, GAP Inc. and cities such as Tokyo and Rotterdam.

### Course **Modules**
MODULE 1
**Fundamentals of Climate Resilience**
  * Overview of Climate Change Science
  * Historical Climate Trends and Future Projections
  * Types of Climate Risks
  * What is Climate Resilience
  * Opportunities & Challenges for Businesses
  * Opportunities & Challenges for Cities
  * National and Regional Climate Regulations and Frameworks


MODULE 2
**Financial Impact of Climate Resilience**
  * Potential Financial Risks of Climate Change for Cities & Businesses
  * Methods for Assessing Financial Risks Related to Climate Change
  * Cost-Benefit Analysis of Climate Adaptation Strategies
  * Case Studies
  * Climate Risk Disclosure
  * Sustainability Reporting and Climate Risk Disclosure


MODULE 3
**Climate Resilience Frameworks & Standards**
  * Carbon-related Standards and Frameworks
  * USA: The National Climate Resilience Framework
  * ISO 14091
  * Climate Disclosure Standards Board (CDSB)
  * World Business Council for Sustainable Development (WBCSD)
  * Greenhouse Gas Protocol (GHG Protocol)
  * CDP
  * GRI
  * SASB
  * Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi)


MODULE 4
**Strategies for Integration and Impact**
  * Integration of Climate Risk into Strategic Planning
  * Strategies for Climate Resilience: Adaptations vs Mitigation
  * Case Studies
  * Climate Resilience and Supply Chain Management
  * Strategies for Reducing Carbon Emissions Across Value Chains
  * Use of Technology and Data Analytics for Climate Risk Assessment
  * Collaboration with Stakeholders
  * Ethical and Social Responsibility in Climate Action


MODULE 5
**Advancing Climate Resilience in a Changing World: The Role of Climate Resilient Officer (CRO)**
  * Understanding Complex Climate Systems and Feedback Loops
  * Uncertainty and Risk in Climate Projections
  * Emerging Trends and AI Technologies in Climate Resilience
  * Google Flood Forecasting Initiative: Flood Hub
  * Microsoft AI for Earth Program – Wildfire Detection System
  * Career Opportunities in Climate Resilience and Sustainability
  * Required Skills and Competencies for Climate Resilience Professionals

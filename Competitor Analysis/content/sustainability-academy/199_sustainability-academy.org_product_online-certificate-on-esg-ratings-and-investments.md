###  **About** the course
#### **The course will help you understand**
  1. Meaning and evolution of ESG
  2. How ESG is used by investors
  3. Information on most widely used, globally, ESG ratings: CDP, DJSI, CSRHub, Bloomberg, MSCI, Sustainalytics, CSA
  4. Most recent legislations, such as SFDR, CSRD, EU taxonomy
  5. Responsible Investment and communication


#### **What you get from the course**
  1. Comprehensive introduction and practical knowledge on ESG issues and indicators, standards and ratings
  2. Case studies and videos
  3. CSE Annual Research: ESG Ratings and Sustainability Reporting Trends
  4. Recorded Webinar: How ESG Disclosures Can Support Investors’ Decisions and A Sustainable Economy
  5. Certification by CSE, accredited by CPD
  6. Badge issued by Credly

### Course **Modules**
MODULE 1
**What is ESG and Relative Legislation**
  * ESG Definition
  * The Evolution of ESG
  * Why Report on ESG Considerations
  * Key Considerations for Companies and Investors
  * What can Companies and Investors Do
  * Relating Legislation
  * Investor Initiatives and Upcoming Trends
  * The Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)
  * The European Commission’s Omnibus package


MODULE 2
**Relationship Between Sustainability Reporting and Investors**
  * Integrating Sustainability into Financial Valuation
  * Socially Responsible Investing
  * Guidelines and tools for Identifying ESG Factors
  * The United Nations Environment Programme Finance Initiative


MODULE 3
**Material ESG Issues and Indicators**
  * ESG Indicators
  * Environmental Concerns and Trends
  * Social Concerns and Trends
  * Governance Concerns and Trends


MODULE 4
**ESG Standards & Ratings**
  * ESG Standards & ESG Ratings
  * What is an ESG Rating?
  * How do Investors use ESG Ratings?
  * Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB)
  * Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD)
  * IFRS Standards
  * TCFD transition to IFRS
  * Science-based targets
  * Principles for Responsible Investing (PRI)
  * Dow Jones Sustainability Indices (DJSI)
  * FTSE ESG Ratings
  * Sustainalytics
  * Corporate Sustainability Assessment (CSA)
  * Bloomberg ESG Criteria
  * CDP (Carbon Disclosure Project)


MODULE 5
**Value Creation and How to Communicate with your Financial Stakeholders**
  * Understanding Investor Needs and Interests
  * Company Actions for Enhanced Communication
  * Responsible Investment
  * The Purpose of an Executive Summary
  * What Belongs and What to Avoid in an Executive Summary

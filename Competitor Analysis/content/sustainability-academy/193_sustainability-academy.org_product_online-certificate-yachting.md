# Online Certificate on Sustainability for Yachting Professionals
The Online Certificate on Sustainability for Yachting Professionals course provides yachting professionals with essential knowledge on sustainability, ESG principles, and best practices in the industry. It equips professionals with practical tools to integrate sustainability into yachting operations, ensuring compliance and industry leadership.
CSE has trained and certified over 200 Professionals in the Yachting Industry from Europe, US and other countries, including leading companies such as AkzoNobel, Heesen Yachts, Oceanco, Feadship and Lürssen Yachts.

  1. Sustainability issues related to the Yachting Industry

### Course **Modules**
Module 1
**SUSTAINABILITY (ESG) AND THE BUSINESS CASE FOR ADOPTION**
  * Definition of Terms
  * Why ESG Matters for the Yachting Industry
  * Benefits
  * Yachting Sustainability Material Topics
  * Sustainability (ESG) Related Risks
  * Eco Yachts Timeline


Module 2
**CURRENT GLOBAL AND LOCAL SUSTAINABILITY TRENDS**
  * Circular Economy
  * Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
  * SDGS: Goal 14 Life Below Water Targets
  * SDGs: Exploring Maritime Opportunities (DNV- GL)
  * COP 21 (Paris 2015)
  * International Maritime Organization (IMO)
  * IMO: Reducing greenhouse gas emissions from ships
  * IMO: Action Plan to address marine plastic litter from ships
  * EU Regulations Affecting the Yachting Industry
  * The Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)
  * US Regulations Affecting the Yachting Industry
  * US Clean Water Act (CWA)


Module 3
**SUSTAINABILITY STRATEGY AND RELATED GLOBAL STANDARDS & GUIDELINES**
  * Business Case- Yacht Framework
  * Stakeholder Prioritization – Understanding Relationships & Influence
  * How to successfully apply and integrate sustainability
  * Sustainability Strategy
  * Sustainability Reporting Guidelines for the Superyacht Industry
  * Carbon Management Hierarchy Model
  * Life Cycle Assessment
  * Social Impact Assessment in Yachting Industry
  * Case Studies


Module 4
**SUSTAINABILITY REPORTING FRAMEWORKS AND COMMUNICATION**
  * Guidelines and Rating Indices on Sustainability
  * Sustainability Reporting
  * Sustainability Reporting Regulation Trends
  * GRI Standards
  * ESRS Standards
  * IFRS Introduction
  * Communicating your Report
  * Greenwashing
  * Sustainability Communication Guidelines
  * Tips for Sustainability Integration

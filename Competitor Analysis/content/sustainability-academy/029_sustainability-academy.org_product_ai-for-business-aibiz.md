###  **About** the course
#### **What You will Learn**
  * Understand core **AI concepts and terminology**
  * Discover how AI enhances **user experiences, data analysis, and operational efficiency**
  * Explore how AI is used in **segmenting ESG data, optimizing environmental processes, and securing assets**
  * Identify **design, data, and risk requirements** for implementing AI
  * Learn how to **develop an AI strategy aligned with Sustainability objectives**


#### **What You Get From the Course**
  * Access to **self-paced content** (approx. 5–6 hours to complete)

  * A **digital badge via Credly** upon successful exam completion
  * Case studies and examples focused on **climate, ESG reporting, and sustainability strategy**

  * Leaders seeking to align innovation with **sustainability goals**

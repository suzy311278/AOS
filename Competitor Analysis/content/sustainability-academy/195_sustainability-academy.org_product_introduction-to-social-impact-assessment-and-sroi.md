### Course **Modules**
MODULE 1
**Introduction & Social Impact Assessment**
  * Definition, History and Objectives of Social Impact Assessment
  * Causes and Types of Social Impact
  * Benefits of Social Impact Assessment
  * Process & Methodologies of Social Impact Assessment
  * Principles of Social Impact Assessment
  * SVI (Social Value International) Standards
  * EU Social Taxonomy


MODULE 2
**Causes and Types of Social Impact**
  * Who & When to use SROI
  * The Principles of SROI
  * What to Consider
  * The Stages of SROI
  * Case Studies and Examples


MODULE 3
**Establishing Scope and Identifying Stakeholders**
  * Scope of SROI Analysis
  * Principles of Social Impact Assessment
  * Materiality in SROI
  * Planning & Resource Requirements
  * Involvement Process (Who & When to Communicate)
  * Monitoring and Feedback
  * Case Studies and Examples


MODULE 4
**Impacts & Outcomes**
  * The Impact Map
  * Identifying & Valuing Inputs
  * Establishing Impact
  * Attribution
  * Drop-off
  * Deadweight
  * Case Studies and Examples


MODULE 5
**Reporting**
  * Reporting to Stakeholders
  * Communication & Commitment
  * Assurance
  * SROI and ESG Reporting

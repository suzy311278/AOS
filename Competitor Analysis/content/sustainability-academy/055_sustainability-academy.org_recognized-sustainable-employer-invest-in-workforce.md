Recognized Sustainable Employer: Invest in Workforce
**Becoming Recognized on Sustainability and ESG means that your workforce has gained knowledge and skills on core sustainability issues, as well as ability to apply those skills on the job.**
The recognition provides a Certificate and a badge to companies that are committed to walk the talk on Sustainability and educating their C-Suite Executives, managers and all Staff.

## How your organization can become recognized?
![small blue line](https://sustainability-academy.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/small-line-blue.svg)
### OPTION 01
At least 50% of your employees (and/ or C-Suite Executives or Managers) from each level should attend at least 1 certified self-paced course from Sustainability Academy within a calendar year.
### OPTION 02
At least 70% of your employees (and/or C-Suite Executives or Managers) from each level should attend a *tailor made group course (6h min duration and at least 20 licenses) provided by CSE within a calendar year.  
*Additional fee for course development.

## Fees
![small blue line](https://sustainability-academy.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/small-line-blue.svg)
There are no fees for the Recognition you will received. The only fees you have to take care are those related to the number of Training licenses you will acquire for your staff and or C-Suite Executives (The minimum number of licenses you can get are 20 )
## Validity Period
![small blue line](https://sustainability-academy.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/small-line-blue.svg)
The recognition is valid for one year. The badge indicates the year that it was issued. Companies can start from a recognition level (e.g., bronze) and move to the next one every year (e.g. Silver).Retraining of employees every 2 years is recommended because of the ever-changing sustainability landscape and the new legislations/frameworks.

###  **About** the course
#### **The course will help you understand**
  1. The purpose and structure of IFRS S1 (General Requirements for Sustainability-related Financial Disclosures)
  2. The principles of IFRS S2 (Climate-related Disclosures)
  3. Integration of sustainability and financial reporting
  4. Materiality assessment and data integrity
  5. Governance, strategy, risk management, and metrics under IFRS S1 & S2
  6. Climate-related scenario analysis and transition planning
  7. Global ESG regulations and alignment (EU CSRD, UK SRS, US CCDAA, Canada CSDS, Singapore ACRA/SGX)
  8. GRI and IFRS interoperability and best practices
  9. Real-world case studies (Triodos Bank, BHP Group, DBS Bank, Woolworths Group, Iberdrola, Verizon)


#### **What you get from the course**
  1. Gain practical, hands-on insights into how to implement and interpret the IFRS sustainability standards
  2. Case studies, examples and videos
  3. Certification by CSE, accredited by CPD
  4. Badge issued by Credly

### Course **Modules**
MODULE 1
**Introduction to Sustainability Reporting:**
  * Sustainability & ESG Definition
  * ESG vs Sustainability: Two Distinct Perspective
  * What is ESG Sustainability Reporting?
  * Purpose & Benefits of Sustainability (ESG) Reporting
  * Components of a sustainability report
  * Major Sustainability Reporting Standards
  * How to Choose the Right Sustainability Reporting Standard for Your Company
  * ESG Ratings and Investors’ Decisions
  * ESG Reporting Trends


MODULE 2
**Overview of IFRS Sustainability Standards and Global ESG Legislation:**
  * How ISSB Links to Other ESG Regulations
  * IFRS S1 & S2 Timeline
  * IFRS S1 & S2 Structure
  * Relationship Between IFRS S1 and S2
  * Benefits for Companies and Investors
  * Compliance & Transition Reliefs
  * UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
  * Emissions Gap Report and Key Findings
  * EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)
  * UK Sustainability Reporting Standards (SRS)
  * US Climate Corporate Data Accountability Act (CCDAA)
  * Canadian Sustainability Disclosure Standards (CSDS)


MODULE 3
**IFRS S1 and Materiality:**
  * IFRS S1 Core Content
  * [](https://cse.sustainability-academy.org/unit/view/id:63726 "Triodos Bank - IFRS S1 Alignment")IFRS S1 – Governance [](https://cse.sustainability-academy.org/unit/view/id:63728 "IFRS S1 – Governance Example \(Triodos Bank Report\)")[](https://cse.sustainability-academy.org/unit/view/id:63729 "IFRS S1 – Strategy")
  * IFRS S1 – Strategy
  * [](https://cse.sustainability-academy.org/unit/view/id:63731 "IFRS S1 – Risk Management") IFRS S1 – Risk Management
  * IFRS S1 – Metrics and Targets
  * General Requirements of IFRS S1
  * Content Integrity
  * High-Quality & Decision-Useful Information
  * Selecting topics and metrics under IFRS S1
  * Materiality in IFRS S1
  * Materiality Assessment
  * Steps of Materiality Assessment


MODULE 4
**IFRS S2 and climate-related disclosures:**
  * IFRS S2 Core Content
  * GHG Emissions in IFRS S2
  * IFRS S2 Key Disclosures
  * Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Targets
  * Core content differences from IFRS S1
  * IFRS S2 – Governance and Risk Management
  * IFRS S2 – Strategy (Climate-related risks & opportunities)
  * IFRS S2 – Strategy disclosures (Climate-related risks & opportunities)
  * IFRS S2 – Strategy (Climate resilience & scenario analysis)
  * What is Climate-related scenario analysis
  * IFRS S2 – Metrics
  * Cross-Industry Metrics
  * Industry-Based Metrics
  * IFRS S2 – Targets
  * Amendments to IFRS S2


MODULE 5
**GRI – IFRS S1/S2 alignment guide and next steps:**
  * Similarities between GRI and IFRS Standards
  * Key differences
  * Transition Plan Disclosures
  * Adaptation Plan Disclosures
  * Global Adoption
  * Alignment and Interoperability
  * Interoperability considerations for GHG emissions
  * Mapping of requirements in GRI 305 and IFRS S2 related to GHG emissions
  * Will the IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards replace the GRI Standards?
  * Why IFRS Standards Are Becoming Mandatory While GRI Remains Largely Voluntary
  * Why Use GRI Standards If IFRS Reporting Is Mandatory?

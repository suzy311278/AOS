### Course **Modules**
Module 1
**The definition of circular economy**
  * The origins and definition of the circular economy concept
  * Benefits, principles and limitation of circular economy
  * The 9R framework
  * E.U. circular economy ranking
  * North America circular economy initiatives


Module 2
**Legislation for circular economy**
  * Applying standards to circular economy
  * ISO/TC 323, BS 800
  * The Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) and circular economy
  * ISO14001 and circular economy
  * U.S. Plastics Pact
  * Canada Zero Waste vision
  * E.U. Green Deal


Module 3
**How Carbon and Waste Management Supports Circular Economy**
  * Circular economy and carbon management
  * Carbon management hierarchy model
  * Greenhouse gas protocol
  * CDP
  * Circular economy to tackle climate change
  * Waste management: issues at stake, policy changes
  * Plastics initiative


Module 4
**How to Apply Circular Economy into your Organization or a Start-up**
  * Ten steps towards circular economy
  * Designing circular economy
  * Circular packaging
  * Internal market, industry, entrepreneurship and SMEs
  * Methodology to understand, define, make and release circular economy

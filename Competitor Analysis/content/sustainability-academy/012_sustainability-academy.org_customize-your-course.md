Δεν υπάρχουν cookies για εμφάνιση.

Δεν υπάρχουν cookies για εμφάνιση.

### **Customize Your Course**
_Tailored Sustainability & ESG Training for Organizations, Executives, and Teams_
Does Your Team Need More Focused Training?
Does your team need more focused training on concepts related to **Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR), ESG, Smart Sustainability Strategies, the Circular Economy, or Net Zero goals**?
**Sustainability Academy** provides **customized online courses** specifically designed to address your organization’s unique sustainability needs and challenges. We specialize in helping companies **customize their course** to:
  * Align with sector-specific sustainability issues
  * Train executive leadership, board members, or entire teams
  * Create internal alignment around ESG principles and SDG goals
  * Meet investor, regulatory, and reporting expectations


### Who We Are
**Sustainability** Academy is a global leader in online training for **ESG, sustainability, climate risk, and responsible investing**. With over **25,000 professionals trained across 90+ countries** , we support businesses, non-profits, governments, and universities in building ESG capacity at scale.
We are powered by the **Centre for Sustainability & Excellence (CSE)** and accredited by **CPD UK** , with courses aligned to the **GRI Standards** , the **SDGs** , and major ESG frameworks.
Our approach is practical, flexible, and impact-driven—giving you a chance to **customize your course** and empower your team to lead change.
### **Custom Training for Your Sector**
####  **Sustainability Academy** delivers **custom ESG and sustainability training** for:
  * Executive Board Members & Directors
  * Sustainability Officers & ESG Teams
  * HR & L&D Departments
  * Procurement, Legal, and Finance Departments
  * Industry Associations & Academic Institutions


### What You Can Customize
When you **customize your course** , you can:
  * Select from 50+ modules: ESG ratings, Net Zero strategy, Climate risk, SDG integration, stakeholder engagement
  * Tailor content to your industry: finance, manufacturing, energy, tech, education, government
  * Choose delivery format: self-paced online, live virtual workshops, or hybrid
  * Co-brand certificates & dashboards for internal reporting
  * Include sector-specific case studies and compliance insights


### **Customize Our Flagship Courses**
Transform any of our core courses into a private, organization-specific training solution:

  * [Online Diploma on Sustainable Supply Chain Management](https://sustainability-academy.org/product/diploma-on-sustainable-supply-chain-management/)


We’ll help you customize each course to address your industry, regulatory environment, and internal sustainability maturity.
